## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Increase impressions + rankings for pages stuck at the bottom of Page 1 or top of Page 2.
- **Hidden Demand:** Spray bottles and trigger hardware.
- **Action:** People searching for "spray bottles NZ" are often commercial cleaners looking for bulk deals. Update the `/product/spray-bottles/` page to include tiered pricing keywords or "Commercial Grade Spray Bottles & Triggers."

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Spray Bottles Product Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Commercial Spray Bottles & Triggers NZ | Citrus Based Cleaners`
- **Meta Description:** `Durable, commercial-grade spray bottles and replacement triggers. Perfect for diluting your natural citrus cleaners. Fast shipping across NZ.`

**2. Citrus Concentrate Product Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Eco-Friendly Citrus Based Cleaner NZ | 100% Natural Concentrate`
- **Meta Description:** `Tough on grease, gentle on the planet. Shop our top-rated, 100% natural citrus based cleaner concentrate. Made in New Zealand.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** In the eco-cleaning space, Google (and users) trust safety data sheets (SDS) and "Made in [Country]" guarantees.
- **Action:** Ensure your `/product-information/` page prominently links to downloadable SDS PDFs and a massive "Proudly Made in NZ" badge sits in your header/footer.

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Content Gap Attack):**
To capture top-of-funnel eco-conscious traffic, publish these highly-searched informational articles:
1. *How to Dilute Citrus Cleaner Concentrate for Everyday Household Use*
2. *Top 5 Natural Alternatives to Bleach for Kiwi Homes*
3. *Why Commercial Grade Spray Bottles Last Longer (And Save You Money)*
4. *How to Safely Clean Bike Chains Using Natural Citrus Degreaser*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** Homepage (`/`).
- **Recipient Pages:** `/product/spray-bottles/` and `/product/replacement-triggers-for-spray-bottle/`.
- **Exact Implementation:** Add a "Featured Accessories" section to the homepage explicitly linking to your spray bottles with exact match anchor text: `Buy Spray Bottles NZ`.
