Before you visit

Not sure what your scrap is worth?

These are the two questions customers usually need answered before they bring scrap in or ask about a commercial collection.

How do I know if I am getting the right price?

Your final payment depends on the metal grade, weight, cleanliness and inspection at the yard. Scrap Mart weighs and grades material before payment, so clean separated copper, cable, aluminium, brass and batteries can be priced more accurately than mixed or contaminated loads.

What if I am not sure what metal or grade I have?

Call your nearest branch or send details before visiting. The team can help identify common grades such as bright copper, PVC copper wire, aluminium extrusion, stainless steel, brass, electric motors, lead batteries and mixed steel.

Not sure about the grade? Use the enquiry form and include photos or a short description when the site is connected.

Common questions

Scrap metal questions

Useful answers for Google search, AI-search visibility and customers who need a quick decision before calling.

What scrap metal does Scrap Mart buy?

Scrap Mart buys copper, copper cable, batteries, lead, aluminium, brass, stainless steel, steel, electric motors, radiators, hot water cylinders, air conditioners, heat pumps, zinc and other accepted metal grades.

Does Scrap Mart show scrap metal prices online?

Yes. Scrap Mart shows scrap metal prices by material and grade, with pricing managed through the website and updated regularly.

Can I get paid the same day?

Yes. Once your material has been inspected, weighed and confirmed, Scrap Mart offers same-day payment by cash or bank transfer.

Can Scrap Mart pick up scrap metal?

Scrap Mart can help with pickup enquiries for suitable commercial volumes. Collection depends on material type, quantity, location, access and commercial value.

Where is Scrap Mart located?

Scrap Mart has an Auckland yard at 11 Edinburgh Street, Onehunga, and a Christchurch branch at 48 Curries Road, Hillsborough, Christchurch 8022.

Does Scrap Mart buy scrap batteries?

Yes. Scrap Mart buys scrap batteries at both Auckland and Christchurch. Used batteries are sold only from the Auckland branch.
