# Scrap Mart Content Rules

Last updated: 26 June 2026.

## Voice

Use practical, plain, New Zealand business-owner language.

Tone:

- direct
- helpful
- transparent
- trade-friendly
- professional but not corporate

Avoid:

- fluffy sustainability claims without proof
- overdone "we are the best" wording
- generic recycling copy that could belong to any competitor
- technical scrap jargon without a simple explanation

## Homepage Rules

The homepage should not start with household scrap.

Lead with:

- Scrap metal buyers in Auckland and Christchurch
- competitive weekly prices
- copper, batteries, aluminium, cables, and commercial scrap
- Onehunga yard and Hillsborough Christchurch branch
- same-day payment
- certified weighing
- pickup and commercial collection pathways

## Pricing Rules

- Mention weekly price updates clearly.
- Add "last updated" near every price table.
- Explain why grades affect price.
- Tell users to separate clean materials for stronger returns.
- Avoid promising a price before inspection.

## Location Rules

- Auckland and Onehunga can be written as confirmed.
- Christchurch should be written as a confirmed branch at 48 Curries Road, Hillsborough, Christchurch 8022.
- Do not use "opening soon" wording in the live website plan.

## Compliance Rules

Mention only approved compliance proof:

- secondhand dealer licence
- certified scales/weighbridge
- NZIML
- NZ EPA permits
- AMR membership

AMR/NZAMR logo has been supplied by the client. Do not display other licence or certification logos until permission is confirmed.

## Not Accepted / Sensitive Items

Do not highlight:

- TVs
- solar panel recycling
- e-waste as a homepage or lead service, even though selected e-waste items are listed as POA in the supplied price list
- hazardous waste beyond confirmed battery handling

Confirmed prohibited items:

- asbestos
- oil
- lithium batteries
- alkaline batteries
- bio waste
- gas bottles
- flammable liquids

Add a future FAQ section for items Scrap Mart does not accept.

## Service Rules

- Do not include bin services in the current website structure. The client asked for the Bin Services & Sizes section to be removed.
- Do not target car bodies, car engines, or machinery.
- Scrap Mart buys scrap batteries at both Auckland and Christchurch.
- Used batteries are sold only from Auckland.
- Use "Free scrap metal drop-off for household appliances" where household-appliance drop-off is relevant.
