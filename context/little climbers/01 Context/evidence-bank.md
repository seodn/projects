- real photos of each centre, rooms, playgrounds, meals, and vans
- approved teacher/team photos
- approval to mention teacher cultural backgrounds
- exact staff qualification counts or percentages by centre
- total children served since 2019
- current ERO reports or licence links for each centre

## Claims To Avoid Until Supported

- "best daycare in Auckland"
- "highest rated"
- "award-winning"
- "state-of-the-art"
- "all teachers are fully qualified"
- "free transport at every centre"
- "all meals included at every centre"
- "open 7am-8pm"
- "infant care at Pukekohe"
- exact fee savings or discount values unless current and approved

## Review And Proof Strategy

For SEO and AI search, proof should be organised by centre:

- centre-specific reviews
- centre-specific photos
- centre-specific staff/team evidence
- centre-specific school/community links
- centre-specific FAQs

This will make location pages more useful than competitors that use broad corporate copy.
