# Scrap Mart Competitor Notes

Last updated: 26 June 2026.

## Competitor Summary

Primary competitor:

- Endless Metals - strongest online competitor for Onehunga/Auckland, pricing transparency, reviews, metal-grade pages, and brand polish.

Secondary competitors:

- Phoenix Recycling - national-scale recycler with strong service pages, compliance, collection/bin services, and broad location coverage.
- National Steel - useful comparator for services and Christchurch presence, but not as strong a direct commercial positioning match.
- Metalcorp - important Christchurch competitor/reference for public prices, service detail, and metal grading information.

## Endless Metals

Website: https://www.endless.co.nz/

Strengths:

- Strong homepage positioning: "Auckland's Highest Rated Scrap Metal & Copper Recycler".
- Clear live pricing pages by location.
- Pricing calculator.
- Metal-grade pages for copper, aluminium, brass, steel, batteries, stainless, motors, and more.
- 1,700+ Google review claim on homepage.
- Two Auckland locations: Onehunga and Wairau Valley.
- Clear trust logos and licence signals.
- Price-match promise by location.
- Tradie-focused loyalty programme.

Weaknesses/opportunity for Scrap Mart:

- Scrap Mart can compete by being more practical, trade-first, and locally clear.
- Scrap Mart can emphasise fast yard experience, direct owner/team trust, and Christchurch launch.
- Scrap Mart should avoid copying Endless, but should match the quality of pricing transparency and grade education.

## Phoenix Recycling

Website: https://www.phoenixrecycling.co.nz/

Strengths:

- National footprint with many North Island and South Island locations.
- Strong collection and bin service pages.
- Good compliance and sustainability positioning.
- Covers metal recycling, battery recycling, e-waste, whiteware, metallic packaging, solar panel recycling, demolition, energy sector, export and brokerage.
- Strong fleet and dangerous goods handling claims.

Weaknesses/opportunity for Scrap Mart:

- Phoenix is a large national operator, so Scrap Mart can position as easier, local, direct, and price-transparent.
- Scrap Mart should not try to match Phoenix's national breadth. It should win on Auckland/Christchurch local relevance and weekly prices.

## National Steel

Website: https://www.nationalsteel.co.nz/services

Strengths:

- Multi-location business including Auckland and Christchurch.
- Clear service list: public drive-through, factory bin service, dealer collections, destruction service, drop-off, scrap pickup.
- Christchurch page confirms the wider Christchurch market has active scrap metal competition.
- Exports page gives confidence around processing and export capability.

Weaknesses/opportunity for Scrap Mart:

- Website content is thinner and less modern.
- Pricing page is enquiry-based, not a full live price table.
- Scrap Mart can beat them on price transparency, modern UX, metal grade education, and clearer trade pathways.

## Metalcorp

Website: https://www.metalcorp.co.nz/

Strengths:

- Strong Christchurch credibility.
- Family-owned positioning and 30 years in the industry.
- Public price lists by location.
- Useful metal grading education.
- Clear services page with sell direct, collection service, skips, job sighting, and public weighbridge.

Weaknesses/opportunity for Scrap Mart:

- Christchurch location is Hornby, while Scrap Mart's confirmed branch is in Hillsborough.
- Scrap Mart can build a stronger Christchurch landing page around 48 Curries Road, Hillsborough, and use weekly prices as a conversion hook.
- Metalcorp's public price list is strong, but the user experience is dated. Scrap Mart can make pricing easier to scan and update.
