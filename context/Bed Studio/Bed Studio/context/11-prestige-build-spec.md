# Bed Studio Prestige Build Specification

Updated: 29 July 2026

## Build Rule

Prestige is the shortlisted full-site theme. Build it as an unpublished theme and keep the current Horizon/Radiant holding site live until content, mobile, SEO, tracking and purchase-path QA are complete.

## Main Navigation

Keep the desktop and mobile menu to six top-level items and no more than two levels:

1. Mattresses
2. Beds & Bases
3. Headboards
4. SleepMatch
5. Newmarket Showroom
6. Guides & Support

Use Size, Feel and Sleeper Type as collection filters and secondary menu links. Keep foam/spring construction in product specifications or a secondary filter.

## Homepage Section Order

1. Premium Newmarket hero with showroom and shop CTAs.
2. Shop mattresses, beds and bases, headboards, storage and adjustable.
3. SleepMatch: choose by Size, Feel and Sleeper Type.
4. NZ-made/customisable proof where supported.
5. Featured premium products.
6. Why visit the Newmarket showroom.
7. Delivery, assembly, finance and warranty trust points once confirmed.
8. Customer proof only when reviews are approved.
9. Sleep guides and FAQs.
10. Showroom map, address, phone and contact CTA.

## Product Template

Every approved product should include:

- clear Bed Studio title and premium summary
- who it suits
- Feel and Sleeper Type guidance
- size and variant options
- construction information
- NZ-made/customisable wording only where supported
- dimensions and materials
- delivery/lead-time information
- warranty and returns information
- showroom CTA
- related products and guides
- unique SEO title and description
- clean image alt text without Budget Beds references

## SleepMatch Guardrail

SleepMatch is a guided shopping tool, not medical advice. Use wording such as `may suit` and encourage customers to test products in the Newmarket showroom. Do not promise pain relief, treatment or a guaranteed sleep outcome.
