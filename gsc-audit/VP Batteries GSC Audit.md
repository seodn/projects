## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Push your high-margin `/mobile-battery-services/` page from Page 2 to Page 1.
- **Hidden Demand:** Emergency roadside battery replacement in Christchurch.
- **Action:** Update the H1 on the mobile services page to "24/7 Mobile Car Battery Replacement Christchurch". 

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Homepage**
- **Title Tag:** `VP Batteries | Premium Car Batteries Christchurch`
- **Meta Description:** `Christchurch's leading supplier of automotive, deep cycle, and commercial batteries. Shop online or book our fast mobile battery replacement service today.`

**2. Mobile Services Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Mobile Car Battery Replacement Christchurch | We Come to You`
- **Meta Description:** `Stuck with a dead battery? Our expert technicians provide fast, reliable mobile car battery replacement across Christchurch. We supply and fit. Call now!`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** In the breakdown/emergency service space, Google (and stressed users) prioritize speed, availability, and clear contact methods.
- **Action:** Make sure your phone number is massive, sticky, and clickable on mobile devices. Add a "Fast Dispatch Across Christchurch" badge/banner to the header.

**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** Homepage (`/`).
- **Recipient Pages:** `/mobile-battery-services/` and `/product-category/deep-cycle/`.
- **Exact Implementation:** The homepage has 15,000+ impressions. It must act as a funnel. Add a massive, distinct section above the fold: "Need it Fitted Now? Book Mobile Battery Replacement in Christchurch." Link this explicitly to the mobile services page.
