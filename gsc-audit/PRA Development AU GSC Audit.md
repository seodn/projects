## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Increase impressions + rankings for pages stuck on Page 2 and 3.
- **Hidden Demand:** Queries like `3 bedroom house for sale perth` (Pos 16.3) and `residential property developers perth` (Pos 18.9) are stuck on Page 2. 
- **Action:** These 3 actions will increase traffic fastest in the AU market:
  1. Add a dedicated section on the Homepage specifically for "Residential Property Developers in Perth", outlining your track record.
  2. Map 3 contextual internal links with the anchor "3 bedroom houses for sale Perth" pointing to `/perth-3-bedroom-properties` from your active listings.
  3. Expand the H2 headings on `/perth-investment-properties` to specifically mention "Perth Rental Yields" and "Vacancy Rates".

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** Google trusts this domain for "Property Developer" queries much more than for individual "House for sale" queries. Your homepage is ranking strongly for `property developers perth` (Pos 10.3).
- **Action:** Lean into this trust by building out a "Company Track Record" or "Completed Projects Portfolio" to solidify your authority as a premium Perth developer.

## Additional Intelligence Layers

### 1. Business Value & Commercial Intent Scoring
Queries like `residential property developers perth` score a **9.5/10** for Lead Potential (B2B / High Net Worth). These users are looking for track records, feasibility studies, and industry awards, not generic text about "First time buyers."

### 2. Implementation Readiness & Execution Intelligence
- **Exact Missing Entities:** The `/perth-investment-properties` page is missing the entities: *Rental Yields, Vacancy Rates, Feasibility Studies, Strata vs Freehold, Depreciation Schedules*.
- **Exact FAQ Suggestion:** Add: *"Why are Perth investment properties seeing such high rental demand right now?"*
- **Exact Heading Recommendation:** Change `Ideal Buyers for Investment Properties` to `Why Smart Investors Are Targeting the Perth Market`.

### 3. Semantic Internal Link Modelling & Authority Hub Detection
- **Authority Hub Missing:** Similar to the NZ site, the AU site relies on a flat structure (`/perth-3-bedroom-properties`). You need a centralized `/properties/` hub that acts as a silo, feeding authority down to specific configurations (3-bed, 4-bed) and locations (Rivervale, Belmont).

### 4. Content Format Intelligence
- **Winning Competitors Use:** Case studies of completed developments, interactive feasibility calculators, and profiles of their executive team (to build trust).
- **Your Format:** Standard text blocks and generic icons. 
- **Recommendation:** Add a "Completed Projects" visual grid to the `/perth-investment-properties` page to prove your track record immediately upon landing.

### 5. User Journey Modelling
- **Current Flow:** User searches `property developers perth` → Lands on Homepage → Must hunt for investment specifics.
- **Ideal Flow:** User lands on Homepage → Immediately sees a clear division: "For Home Buyers" vs "For Investors & Developers" → Clicks Investors → Sees Feasibility data & Track Record.

### 6. Index Quality Engine (Index Hygiene Analysis)
- **Soft Duplicate URLs:** The `/?utm_source=google-business` URL is ranking (Pos 7.29) and stealing impressions from the clean root domain. Ensure strict canonical tags are in place.
- **Thin Indexed Pages:** URLs like `/belmont-investment-properties` and `/perth-5-bedroom-properties` are getting single-digit impressions and zero clicks. They lack semantic depth. Consider merging them into broader hubs if you cannot add 500+ words of unique value to them.

### 7. Site Quality Scoring (Perth Market)
- **Crawl Quality:** 9/10
- **Index Hygiene:** 6/10 (UTM parameter cannibalization).
- **Semantic Depth:** 4/10 (Missing critical Perth-specific economic data and development jargon).
- **Intent Alignment:** 5/10 (Ranking for developer queries but offering standard consumer messaging).
- **CTR Efficiency:** 3/10 (Bleeding clicks due to lack of SERP trust signals).

### 8. Content-to-Title Consistency Analysis
- Your current titles say "Investment Properties" but the page content feels like a generic template for selling houses. To rank on Page 1 in Australia for investment terms, the page *must* talk about the financials (ROI, Yield, Capital Growth).

### 11. Competitor Sitemap & Content Gap Engine
- **Competitors:** *Finbar, Satterley, Summit Developments, Edge Visionary Living*
- **Sitemap Discovery:** The top Perth competitors dominate by having massive "Investor Centers" or "News & Insights" hubs. They publish monthly updates on the Perth property market, vacancy rates, and infrastructure projects (like METRONET).
- **Output:** To compete for `property developers perth`, PRA Developments must stop looking like a standard real estate agent and start looking like an authority. Build an **"Investor Center"** hub publishing Perth market data to capture top-of-funnel search intent.
