## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Push your high-margin treatments (Crowns, Extractions) from Page 3 to Page 1.
- **Hidden Demand:** Wider Auckland searches for specific treatments.
- **Action:** Your treatment pages are likely generic. Update the H1 on the `/tooth-extraction/` page from "Tooth Extractions" to "Gentle Tooth Extractions in South Auckland". We need to broaden the entity from just "Otahuhu" to "South Auckland / Auckland" for these specific services.

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Homepage**
- **Title Tag:** `Otahuhu Dental Care | Trusted Local Dentist & WINZ Quotes`
- **Meta Description:** `Looking for an affordable dentist in Otahuhu? We provide gentle, professional dental care, emergency extractions, and WINZ quotes. Book online today.`

**2. Tooth Extractions Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Tooth Extractions Auckland | Gentle Emergency Dental Care`
- **Meta Description:** `Suffering from toothache? We offer gentle, safe, and affordable tooth extractions in South Auckland. Emergency appointments and WINZ quotes available.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** In the healthcare space (YMYL - Your Money or Your Life), Google strictly requires localized trust and practitioner authority.
- **Action:** Embed your Google Maps location directly on the Homepage and WINZ page. Ensure your `/contact-us/` page includes a photo of the clinic building from the street so Google (and users) know it's a physical, established clinic.

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Content Gap Attack):**
To capture top-of-funnel local traffic looking for financial assistance and emergency care:
1. *How to Apply for a WINZ Dental Grant in Auckland (Step-by-Step)*
2. *Toothache Emergency: What to Do While Waiting for the Dentist*
3. *Do I Need a Root Canal or an Extraction? Signs & Symptoms*
4. *How Much Does a Dental Crown Cost in NZ? (2024 Guide)*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** `/winz-quotes/` (4,400 Impressions).
- **Recipient Pages:** `/tooth-extraction/` and `/dental-crowns/`.
- **Exact Implementation:** The WINZ page is a powerhouse. Add a section to this page stating: "We provide WINZ quotes for major procedures including [Emergency Tooth Extractions] and [Dental Crowns]." Hyperlink the bracketed text directly to those specific treatment pages to pump ranking authority into them.
