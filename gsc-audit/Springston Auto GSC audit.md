## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Stop bleeding impressions to generic queries and capture high-intent local traffic.
- **Hidden Demand:** Mechanics and WOFs in Springston AND Rolleston.
- **Action:** Update the Homepage H1 from generic text to "Trusted Local Mechanic for Springston & Rolleston". We must anchor the entity to these two specific geographic locations.

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Homepage**
- **Title Tag:** `Springston Auto | Expert Mechanics for Springston & Rolleston`
- **Meta Description:** `Looking for a reliable mechanic? Springston Auto provides expert car servicing, WOFs, and repairs for the Springston and Rolleston communities. Book today!`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** In the automotive repair space, Google trusts MTA (Motor Trade Association) badges, clear pricing, and verified physical addresses.
- **Action:** Ensure your MTA badge is highly visible above the fold on the homepage. Embed your Google Map showing your proximity to both Springston and Rolleston.

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Listicles for Content Gap Attack):**
To capture top-of-funnel local traffic and build trust with vehicle owners, publish these highly-clickable listicles:
1. *7 Warning Signs Your Car Needs a Brake Check Immediately*
2. *Top 5 Reasons Vehicles Fail Their WOF (And How to Pass)*
3. *10 Essential Winter Car Maintenance Tips for Canterbury Drivers*
4. *5 Strange Car Noises You Should Never Ignore*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** Homepage (`/`).
- **Recipient Pages:** Specific service pages (WOF, Servicing).
- **Exact Implementation:** Replace the generic "Our Services" button on the homepage with a specific grid of services. Link directly to a `/wof-inspections/` page and a `/car-servicing/` page using exact match text.
