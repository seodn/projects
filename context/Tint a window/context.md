## Key Explanations
Daytime privacy:
Mirror film can provide privacy when outside is brighter than inside. It does not provide the same privacy at night or on dull days when lights are on inside.

Full privacy:
Frosted or opaque film still lets in natural light but prevents seeing in or out.

Fading:
Fading is not only UV. Visible light and heat also contribute, so the site should explain fading protection in plain terms.

Blinds vs film:
Blinds block light and remove views. Window film can help manage UV, glare, heat, and privacy while preserving the outlook where the right film is selected.

Film failure concern:
Customers may worry about bubbling, peeling, or flaking. Copy should address this directly with modern film technology, recommended products, and warranty-backed installation.

## Objections And Responses
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Will the film bubble, peel, or flake? | Modern film technology has improved significantly. Tint A Window should explain product quality, manufacturer warranties, and installation warranties clearly. |
| Will it make my room dark? | The right film can soften and balance light rather than darken the space. Film choice depends on privacy, heat, glare, and outlook goals. |
| Can I still see out? | Many films preserve the view. Frosted/opaque films provide full privacy but block clear views. Explain this upfront by film type. |
| Does mirror film give privacy at night? | Mirror film is mainly daytime privacy. When it is darker outside and brighter inside, people can see in. Frosted film gives privacy day and night but you cannot see through it. |
| What warranty applies? | Warranty information needs to be confirmed by film type, internal vs external film, and chosen brand. LLumar is the recommended/default film; customer-requested alternatives may have different warranty limits. |
| Why not use blinds or curtains? | Blinds and curtains block light and views and may still allow heat issues. Window film can protect from UV/glare/heat while keeping the outlook. |
| Can I use another brand, like 3M? | Tint A Window can source/use requested brands in some cases, but warranty and recommendation should be explained. LLumar is the preferred film for NZ conditions. |
