# BedStudio Launch Plan

## Launch Principle

The website should not wait for a perfect catalogue. Launch a credible landing page first, then build out products and categories in stages.

## Timeline From 9 July Meeting

Working targets discussed:

- 13 July 2026: start focused website work.
- 29-30 July 2026: aim to complete the full website.
- 31 July 2026 / 1 August 2026: target launch campaign timing.

These are internal working targets, not public promises.

## Website Build Sequence

1. Use confirmed GBP address and phone; confirm brand spelling, domain, email, hours, Google Maps link, and parking/access notes.
2. Trial Prestige Shopify theme.
3. Confirm theme purchase after visual check.
4. Connect domain.
5. Build landing page/homepage.
6. Add categories and navigation.
7. Import product data as draft products.
8. Rewrite priority product copy.
9. Build delivery, warranty, returns, finance, and FAQ pages.
10. Add local SEO basics and schema.
11. Add analytics/conversion tracking.
12. QA mobile and desktop.
13. Publish staged product/category pages.

## Landing Page Minimum

Landing page should include:

- BedStudio logo.
- Premium Newmarket positioning.
- What the store sells.
- Main categories.
- NZ-made/customisable message.
- Showroom/contact details: 25B Broadway, Newmarket, Auckland 1023; 09 972 4142.
- Launch offer if approved.
- Delivery/payment/warranty trust strip.
- Call/contact CTA.
