# Homepage Content Brief

Version: 0.1

Approver: Vaughan Perry

Status: ready for draft copy, not final publication

## 1. Target keyword and cluster

●        Primary: car batteries Christchurch.

●        Supporting: battery shop Christchurch; mobile battery service Christchurch; automotive batteries Christchurch; battery fitting Christchurch; trade batteries Christchurch.

## 2. Intent and format

Local commercial and urgent transactional. The page should behave like a local service homepage, not a catalogue dump.

## 3. Audience

●        A driver with a flat or failing battery.

●        A planned retail buyer.

●        A workshop, car yard or fleet buyer.

## 4. Job to be done

Help the visitor quickly decide whether to call for mobile help, visit the Sydenham workshop, find a battery or enquire about trade supply.

## 5. Depth

Approximately 900 to 1,200 useful words, plus product cards and FAQs.

## 6. Section outline

### Hero

●        H1: Car batteries and mobile battery help in Christchurch.

●        Short value line: local owners, correct-fit advice, workshop testing, trade and retail.

●        CTAs: Call for help; Find a battery.

●        Secondary: Trade account.

### Urgent help strip

●        Car will not start?

●        Callout availability and approved service-area wording.

●        Separate workshop hours from urgent call availability.

### Choose your path

●        Mobile replacement.

●        Visit for free workshop testing.

●        Shop/find a battery.

●        Trade and wholesale.

### Why VP Batteries

●        Vaughan’s approximately 28 years.

●        Owner-operated Christchurch team.

●        Tests before recommending where appropriate.

●        Sourcing and fitment help.

●        Fair pricing without a “cheapest” claim.

### Product families

Show eight concise category cards, not a full product grid.

### Testing process

Battery, alternator and starter checks.

### Trade supply

Workshops, car yards, fleets, trucks, farm and industrial.

### Mobile service

Home, work and roadside examples, with conditions.

### Recycling

Old lead-acid battery acceptance, with approved evidence only.

### Founder/team

Vaughan and Murray, with real photos.

### Reviews

Only approved, attributable reviews.

### FAQs

●        Do you come to me?

●        Is testing free?

●        How do I know which battery fits?

●        Do you fit the battery?

●        What do you do for hybrid and EV vehicles?

●        Can you take my old battery?

### Final CTA

Call, send vehicle details or request trade supply.

## 7. Required entities and details

VP Batteries; Christchurch; Canterbury; Sydenham; car battery; AGM; EFB; stop-start; CCA; alternator; starter motor; mobile battery replacement; trade supply; battery recycling.

## 8. Internal links

●        Mobile service.

●        Trade and wholesale.

●        Battery testing.

●        Car batteries.

●        Contact.

Inbound links after publish:

●        About.

●        Mobile service.

●        Trade page.

## 9. Proof required

●        Approved address and contacts.

●        Founder experience.

●        Real team and mobile-service photos.

●        Approved reviews.

●        Final service and fee matrix.

## 10. Anti-patterns

●        No generic “number one” or “best in Christchurch”.

●        No guaranteed 24/7 wording.

●        No huge specification table on the homepage.

●        No competitor attacks.

●        No unsupported recycling or manufacturer claim.

## 11. Success criteria

Track calls, battery-finder submissions, mobile-service enquiries, trade enquiries, directions and completed eligible orders. Establish baseline in the first 30 days and review at 30, 60 and 90 days.

## Shared category-page contract

Each category page must include:

●        one clear H1;

●        who the category is for;

●        plain-language selection advice;

●        approved products from the canonical SKU table;

●        key specification explanations;

●        fitment/compatibility warning;

●        price and fulfilment rules;

●        warranty summary linked to the full policy;

●        testing and mobile-service options;

●        3 to 5 specific FAQs;

●        links to related categories and advice;

●        Product and Breadcrumb schema only when accurate.

Target length should follow the actual SERP and product depth. Do not pad pages to a fixed word count.
