# Client-Supplied Keywords From Dharmesh Email

Last updated: 26 June 2026.

Source: Gmail email from Dharmesh Patel, subject `Re: Keywords - New Website`, received 15 June 2026, with attachment `Keywords.docx`.

Use this as an internal SEO and content reference. The list contains useful customer/search language, but many phrases are rough variations, spelling variants, or broad ideas. Do not create one page per keyword. Map them to the existing website structure unless a keyword reveals a confirmed service, metal, or location gap.

Important update: Dharmesh's later email on 24 June 2026 supersedes some of this keyword list. Bin services should be removed from the current website structure. Car bodies, car engines, and machinery should not be targeted. Scrap Mart buys scrap batteries at Auckland and Christchurch, but sells used batteries only from Auckland.

## Structure Impact

The supplied keywords do not require a major change to the website structure.

They mostly reinforce the current plan:

- city pages for Auckland and Christchurch
- city price pages
- What We Buy pages for copper, cable, aluminium, batteries, brass, stainless steel, steel, lead, motors, radiators, air conditioners, and hot water cylinders
- service pages for collection, pickup, commercial scrap, industrial scrap, factory scrap, construction/demolition scrap, regular collection, and household drop-off
- resource pages for pricing, grading, sorting, ID requirements, pickup vs drop-off, valuable metals, and not accepted items

Important guardrails:

- The list includes `Scrap Metal Bin Hire`, but the client later asked to remove bin services from the website structure.
- The list includes `Car Bodies Scrap`, `Car Engines Scrap`, and `Top $$$ for Scrap Metal & Machinery`, but the client later confirmed these should not be targeted.
- The list includes `Used automotive battery price` and `USED AGM Battery Price`; use this carefully because used batteries are sold only from Auckland, while scrap batteries are bought at both locations.
- The list includes `Public Free drop off metal scrap`; use the cleaner client-approved wording "Free scrap metal drop-off for household appliances."

## Recommended Keyword Use

- Use broad city terms on `/locations/auckland` and `/locations/christchurch`.
- Use price terms on `/scrap-metal-prices`, `/scrap-metal-prices/auckland`, and `/scrap-metal-prices/christchurch`.
- Use metal and grade terms on the matching What We Buy pages.
- Use pickup, collection, drop-off, commercial, factory, industrial, and construction terms on service pages. Do not target bin hire in the current structure.
- Use rough question-style phrases such as "How to recycle my metal" in resource articles and FAQs, not as main page titles.
