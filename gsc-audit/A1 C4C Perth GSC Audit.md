## 5 Master Decision Systems

**Purpose:** Increase impressions + rankings for pages stuck in the Page 8/9 blackhole.

- **Action:** Competitors (like Soltoggio Bros and Midland Wreckers) dominate because they list *exact* truck brands. Update your page to explicitly include: "We buy Isuzu, Fuso, UD, Kenworth, Hino, Scania, Volvo, and Mack trucks in any condition." Add a bold "Free Towing Across Perth" badge.
