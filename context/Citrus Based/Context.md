## Product Overview
**One-liner:**
Citrus Based Cleaner is a New Zealand-made concentrated citrus cleaner that replaces cupboards full of cleaning products with one powerful, biodegradable solution.

**What it does:**
Citrus Based Cleaner sells concentrated cleaning products for homes, rural properties, commercial sites, hospitality, workshops, and bike care. The hero product, Original Citrus Concentrate, is diluted with water for everyday cleaning and can also be used stronger for stains, grease, grime, carpets, ovens, windows, bathrooms, vehicles, floors, laundry stains, and many other jobs. The product is based on d-limonene, a natural solvent extracted from orange peel, which gives it strong degreasing performance and a natural citrus smell.

**Product category:**
Eco-friendly multipurpose cleaner, citrus cleaner concentrate, biodegradable cleaner NZ, all-purpose cleaner concentrate, degreaser, septic-safe cleaner, commercial cleaning concentrate.

**Product type:**
E-commerce physical product brand.

**Business model:**
Online sales direct to consumer and commercial buyers, with repeat orders from long-term customers. Products are sold in multiple sizes from smaller trial bottles through bulk sizes. The current website shows Original Citrus Concentrate and Citrus Blast from $30.00-$525.00, Chain Degreaser from $28.00-$75.00, plus accessories such as spray bottles, replacement triggers, and doodle bug pads.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:**
Customers are tired of buying many separate cleaners that are expensive, watered down, harsh-smelling, hard to compare, or not suitable for rural/septic use.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Supermarket multipurpose cleaners are usually pre-diluted, so customers pay for water and need to buy more often.
- Some concentrates require much stronger mixes before they work effectively.
- Many chemical cleaners include harsh ingredients or strong smells customers want to avoid.
- Specialist cleaners create clutter because customers need separate products for kitchens, bathrooms, carpets, ovens, windows, stains, vehicles, and degreasing.
- The current Citrus Based Cleaner product variation flow can hide prices behind a size selector, confusing some customers.

**What it costs them:**
More money spent on watered-down products, more time choosing different cleaners, more storage space, more uncertainty about surfaces and septic safety, and more friction before ordering.

**Emotional tension:**
Customers want a cleaner that simply works. Older customers especially can feel frustrated if pricing or product options are unclear. Rural customers want confidence that a cleaner will not cause problems with septic systems or environmental runoff.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:**
Other citrus cleaner and eco-cleaner concentrate brands in New Zealand and Australia, including Olympic Clean as a referenced comparison site. Olympic Clean separates product sizes as individual products, which may make price and size selection easier for some shoppers.

**Secondary:**
Supermarket multipurpose sprays, Mitre 10-style concentrates, disinfectants, degreasers, carpet cleaners, oven cleaners, window cleaners, and other single-use cleaning products.

**Indirect:**
Customers continuing to buy separate specialised cleaners out of habit, using harsh chemical cleaners because they are familiar, or calling to order instead of buying online because the website feels confusing.

- New Zealand-made in Christchurch and locally owned/operated.
- Original recipe and long manufacturing relationship.
- Concentrated formula: customers add water instead of paying for water.
- Extremely versatile: one product for many household, commercial, rural, automotive, and stain-removal jobs.
- Based on d-limonene from orange peel.
- Free from commonly unwanted harsh ingredients such as ammonia, bleach, phosphates, petrochemicals, benzene, nitrates, and silicone according to current site claims.
- Biodegradable and especially relevant for septic-tank and rural customers.
- Long-term customer loyalty, including customers who have used it for 7-10 years and families using it across generations.

**How we do it differently:**
Lead with the real story: a product sold face-to-face across New Zealand for decades by people who had to prove it worked in real homes, motels, farms, businesses, and workshops. Make the buying experience clear by showing size, price, value, and use case upfront.

**Why that's better:**
The story creates trust, the concentrate creates value, and the product versatility makes it easier for customers to understand why they should buy it instead of another cleaner.

**Why customers choose us:**
They want one cleaner that works across many jobs, is cost-effective over time, smells fresh, avoids harsh chemical ingredients, is suitable for rural/septic situations, and comes from a known New Zealand business with decades of practical use behind it.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I can't find the price." | Make prices visible without forcing customers to understand variation selectors. Consider separate product cards for 500 ml, 1 L, 2 L, 5 L, and bulk sizes. |
| "Why do I have to pay freight?" | Be transparent that freight is expensive, especially rural freight, and the business is already absorbing part of the real shipping cost in many cases. |
| "Which product do I need: Original, Blast, or Chain Degreaser?" | Original is the hero everyday cleaner and rural/septic recommendation. Blast is the sanitising/chloride option introduced during COVID and may need renaming for clarity. Chain Degreaser is the ready-to-use bike/workshop degreaser. |
| "Is it safe for septic tanks/rural use?" | Recommend Original Citrus Concentrate and clearly explain its biodegradable/rural suitability. Avoid recommending Blast where chloride/alcohol-based products are unsuitable. |
| "How much do I mix?" | Put dilution guidance beside each product and size. For everyday use, emphasize very small amounts of concentrate in water, with the spray bottle showing dilution instructions. |

**Anti-persona:**
Customers who only want the cheapest single pre-mixed supermarket spray for one small job and do not care about concentration, versatility, refill value, biodegradable ingredients, or local NZ ownership.

## Switching Dynamics

Current cleaners are watered down, expensive over time, harsh, cluttered, confusing, not septic-friendly, or too specialised.

**Pull:**
One NZ-made concentrated product can clean almost everything, reduce waste, save money, and has 30 years of real-world customer use behind it.

**Habit:**
People keep buying familiar supermarket cleaners or multiple specialised products because they know where to find them and do not have to learn dilution ratios.

**Anxiety:**
Will a concentrate be hard to mix? Will it work as well as stronger chemical cleaners? Is it safe for my surfaces, carpets, septic tank, food areas, kids, pets, or plants? How much freight will cost? Which size should I buy first?

## Website Context And Messaging Implications
- Make the website user-friendly for older customers and first-time buyers. Prices and sizes should be obvious.
- Consider splitting size variations into separate product cards or a clearer size grid to reduce confusion.
- Lead with Original Citrus Concentrate as the hero product.
- Keep Citrus Blast but consider renaming it to a clearer sanitiser/disinfectant-style name if the product remains available.
- Explain the difference between Original, Blast, and Chain Degreaser in a simple comparison table.
- Add a "which product is right for me?" section.
- Add use-case/application pages or tabs such as Domestic, Rural/Septic, Commercial, Automotive, Hospitality, Carpet & Fabric, and Bike/Workshop.
- Add SDS/MSDS downloads, MPI or NZ Food Safety proof, NZ Made license, brochures, and application guides wherever available.
- Use the founder story prominently: travel, door-to-door selling across New Zealand, Fiji, and Australia; becoming sales manager; later buying the company; and serving customers for decades.
- Use real owner photos and real product photos. Avoid overly AI-looking ad creative because customers have commented positively on authentic non-AI ads.
- Add Google review links and/or QR code prompts after purchase and at pickup.
- Be transparent about shipping, especially rural shipping, and set expectations around 2-3 day delivery while noting delays can happen due to weather/ferry issues.
