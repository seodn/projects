## Keyword Guardrails

- Do not target wheel alignment unless the client starts offering it.
- Do not lead with dent repair, panel beating, or bodywork.
- Do not overuse "cheap mechanic"; use fair, transparent, or starting-price language.
- Do not split every suburb into a location page at launch.
- Keep hybrid pages clear and plain for laypeople, not only technicians.
