# Scrap Mart Competitor Audit

Audited: 18 June 2026

## Strategic Summary

The new Scrap Mart website must match the market's move towards transparent pricing, metal-grade education, and strong local pages. Endless is the benchmark for Auckland. Metalcorp is the useful Christchurch pricing benchmark. Phoenix shows what a larger national operator does well around bins, collections, compliance, and locations.

## Endless Metals

Website: https://www.endless.co.nz/

Relevant pages:

- Homepage: https://www.endless.co.nz/
- Pricing: https://www.endless.co.nz/pricing/
- Onehunga prices: https://www.endless.co.nz/pricing-onehunga/
- Copper: https://www.endless.co.nz/copper-recycling-in-auckland/
- Aluminium: https://www.endless.co.nz/aluminium-recycling-auckland/

What they do well:

- Strong H1 and position: Auckland's highest rated scrap metal and copper recycler.
- Pricing is visible and location-specific.
- Onehunga pricing page includes weekly validity dates.
- Price calculator is a strong conversion feature.
- Metal pages explain grades with images and practical examples.
- Clear local yard details and hours.
- Strong review proof and trust badges.

What Scrap Mart should do:

- Build a weekly pricing system from day one.
- Create grade-led metal pages, not generic metal pages.
- Use Scrap Mart's direct team, same-day payment, certified weighing, and Christchurch launch to create a different angle.

## Phoenix Recycling

Website: https://www.phoenixrecycling.co.nz/

Relevant pages:

- Metal recycling: https://www.phoenixrecycling.co.nz/metals
- Battery recycling: https://www.phoenixrecycling.co.nz/batteries
- Collections: https://www.phoenixrecycling.co.nz/collections
- Bin services: https://www.phoenixrecycling.co.nz/bin-services
- Locations: https://www.phoenixrecycling.co.nz/locations

What they do well:

- Broad service architecture.
- Strong collection/bin service detail.
- Good compliance and hazardous material handling signals.
- Large footprint across New Zealand.
- Good "what we recycle" structure.

What Scrap Mart should do:

- Avoid trying to look national.
- Compete on local yard convenience, transparent prices, and personal service.
- Create better conversion flows for trade/commercial customers.

## National Steel

Website: https://www.nationalsteel.co.nz/services

Relevant pages:

- Services: https://www.nationalsteel.co.nz/services
- Materials accepted: https://www.nationalsteel.co.nz/materials-accepted
- Pricing enquiry: https://www.nationalsteel.co.nz/pricing
- Christchurch: https://www.nationalsteel.co.nz/christchurch

What they do well:

- Has Auckland and Christchurch location coverage.
- Lists practical services: public drive-through, factory bin service, dealer collections, destruction, drop-off, pickup.
- Christchurch page confirms the wider Christchurch market has active scrap metal yard competition.

What Scrap Mart should do:

- Build a more modern, search-ready page structure.
- Use public weekly prices, not just pricing enquiry.
- Make the Christchurch page strong from the first build, using the confirmed address at 48 Curries Road, Hillsborough.

## Metalcorp

Website: https://www.metalcorp.co.nz/

Relevant pages:

- Homepage: https://www.metalcorp.co.nz/
- Services: https://www.metalcorp.co.nz/Services/
- Price lists: https://www.metalcorp.co.nz/Price-Lists/
- Christchurch prices: https://www.metalcorp.co.nz/Price-Lists/Christchurch-Price-Lists/
- Metal info: https://www.metalcorp.co.nz/Metal-Info/

What they do well:

- Strong Christchurch credibility.
- Public Christchurch prices.
- Metal info page explains ferrous and non-ferrous grades.
- Services page explains direct yard sales, collections, bins/skips, job sighting, and public weighbridge.

What Scrap Mart should do:

- Use Christchurch pricing expectations as a benchmark.
- Build better UX than Metalcorp while keeping the practical price-list strength.
- Create a Christchurch local page that is more relevant to Scrap Mart's Hillsborough branch.

## Main Gaps Scrap Mart Can Own

- Weekly prices by grade for Auckland and Christchurch.
- Better "what we buy" structure than the current site.
- Stronger commercial/trade messaging than competitors.
- Simpler enquiry flow: price, drop-off, pickup, commercial contract.
- Clear launch page for Christchurch.
- Practical educational guides that answer buyer questions in plain language.
