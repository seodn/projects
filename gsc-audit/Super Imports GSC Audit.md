## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Push your high-margin installation page (`/our-services/car-audio-installations/`) from Page 2 to Page 1.
- **Hidden Demand:** Professional car audio installation in Auckland.
- **Action:** Right now, your site's architecture heavily favors Apple products and e-commerce. You must inject localized workshop intent into the Car Audio Installation page. Update the H1 to "Professional Car Audio Installation Auckland".

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Car Audio Installation Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Car Audio Installation Auckland | Stereos, Subs & Alarms`
- **Meta Description:** `Upgrade your ride with professional car audio installation in Auckland. We supply and fit premium subwoofers, stereos, and speakers from top brands. Book now!`

**2. iPhone Category Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Refurbished Apple iPhones NZ | Unlocked & Warranty`
- **Meta Description:** `Shop premium refurbished iPhones in NZ. Unlocked, fully tested, and backed by our quality warranty. Fast shipping across New Zealand. Buy online today.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** Your dual identity (Phones + Car Audio) confuses Google's semantic understanding of your business.
- **Action:** You must visually and structurally separate these on the homepage. Create two distinct "hero" paths above the fold: "Shop Devices Online" and "Book Auckland Car Audio Install". Add physical photos of your installation bay to prove you aren't just an importer.

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Listicles for Content Gap Attack):**
To capture top-of-funnel traffic across both of your core verticals:
1. *7 Essential Upgrades for the Ultimate Car Audio System in 2024*
2. *Top 5 Reasons Buying a Refurbished iPhone is Better Than Buying New*
3. *10 Questions to Ask Your Car Stereo Installer Before They Start*
4. *5 Signs Your Car's Factory Speakers Are Ready to Blow*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** Homepage (`/`) and Daily Deals.
- **Recipient Pages:** `/our-services/car-audio-installations/`.
- **Exact Implementation:** The homepage has 17,000+ impressions. Use it to rescue the stalled installation page. In the main navigation and explicitly in the homepage footer, link to the installation page using the exact text `Car Audio Installation Auckland`.
