## Header Menu Detail

### Home
Use this as the main brand entry page.

Include:
- Boutique/upmarket positioning
- Residential and commercial pathways
- Main benefits: heat control, privacy, fade reduction, glare reduction, safety, anti-graffiti
- Trust/proof: 35+ years, family-owned, South Island experience
- Featured projects
- Service-area overview
- Testimonials
- Quote CTA

Suggested label:
- Home

### Residential Window Tinting
This should be a top-level menu item with a dropdown.

Dropdown pages:
1. Fade Reduction Window Films
2. Privacy Window Films
3. Heat Control Window Films
4. Anti-Glare Window Films
5. Frosted / Opaque Window Films
6. Safety & Security Window Films

Optional first dropdown item:
- Residential Window Tinting Overview

Use the overview page to explain residential tinting broadly, then send people to the specific problem pages.

Recommended dropdown order:
1. Residential Window Tinting Overview
2. Fade Reduction Window Films
3. Privacy Window Films
4. Heat Control Window Films
5. Anti-Glare Window Films
6. Frosted / Opaque Window Films
7. Safety & Security Window Films

Why this order:
- Fade, privacy, and heat are the strongest residential demand signals from the transcript.
- Safety/security should stay last because it is lower-volume for residential but still worth having.

### Commercial Window Tinting
This should be a top-level menu item with a dropdown.

Dropdown pages:
1. Heat Rejection / Solar Protection Window Films
2. Anti-Glare Window Films
3. Frosted / Opaque & Decorative Window Films
4. Safety & Security Window Films
5. Anti-Graffiti Window Films

Optional first dropdown item:
- Commercial Window Tinting Overview

Recommended dropdown order:
1. Commercial Window Tinting Overview
2. Heat Rejection / Solar Protection Window Films
3. Anti-Glare Window Films
4. Frosted / Opaque & Decorative Window Films
5. Safety & Security Window Films
6. Anti-Graffiti Window Films

Why this order:
- Heat and glare are the main commercial problems discussed.
- Frosted/opaque is important for offices and schools.
- Safety/security and anti-graffiti should remain separate pages.

- Residential Projects

### About
Use this for trust and positioning.

- Roxana taking over day-to-day leadership
- Decades of industry experience

### FAQs
Use this to answer buying objections and improve search coverage.

Include FAQs for:
- Does window film make rooms dark?
- Can I still see outside?
- Does mirror film give privacy at night?
- What is the difference between mirror film and frosted film?
- Will window film bubble, peel, or flake?
- How long does window film last?
- What warranty applies?
- Is window film suitable for double glazing?
- Can film reduce fading?
- Does film reduce heat?
- Does film reduce glare on computer screens?
- How does anti-graffiti film work?
- What happens after I enquire?
- Do you visit Queenstown, Wanaka, Timaru, Oamaru, etc.?
