## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Push `mechanic new lynn` from Position 11 (Page 2) into the Top 5.
- **Hidden Demand:** Specific local mechanic intent.
- **Action:** The difference between Page 2 and Page 1 is trust and exact match semantics. Ensure your Homepage H1 explicitly says "Expert Mechanic in New Lynn". 

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Homepage**
- **Title Tag:** `AR Auto Tech | Top Rated Mechanic & Auto Electrician New Lynn`
- **Meta Description:** `Looking for a trusted mechanic in New Lynn? AR Auto Tech offers expert car servicing, auto electrical repairs, WOFs, and diagnostics. Book your car in today.`

**2. Auto Electrical Page (Create if missing)**
- **Title Tag:** `Auto Electrician New Lynn | Diagnostics & Repairs`
- **Meta Description:** `Expert auto electrical services in New Lynn. From complex diagnostics to battery replacements and wiring repairs, our specialists have you covered.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** Your `/our-gallery/` and `/testimonials/` pages have good CTRs but low impressions. People *want* to see your work and trust you.
- **Action:** Pull the 3 best testimonials and 3 best gallery photos (showing your workshop and team) and embed them directly onto the Homepage. Don't make users click away to find trust signals.

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Listicles for Content Gap Attack):**
To capture top-of-funnel traffic and establish authority on high-margin tuning/electrical work:
1. *7 Signs Your Car Has an Auto Electrical Problem (Don't Ignore These)*
2. *Top 5 Benefits of Performance Tuning Your Vehicle in Auckland*
3. *10 Questions to Ask Before Choosing a Mechanic in New Lynn*
4. *5 Reasons Your Check Engine Light Just Came On*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** Homepage (`/`).
- **Recipient Pages:** `/services/insurance-repairs/` and a new Tuning page.
- **Exact Implementation:** The homepage must link directly to the high-margin service pages (Insurance Repairs, Tuning, Auto Electrical) using exact match anchor text, rather than forcing users through a generic `/our-services/` bottleneck.
