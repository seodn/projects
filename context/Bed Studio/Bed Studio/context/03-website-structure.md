# BedStudio Website Structure

> Production update, 29 July 2026: use `11-prestige-build-spec.md` for the approved working sitemap, navigation and Prestige build requirements. This file remains the earlier strategy record.

## Build Principle

Do not wait for the perfect full catalogue before putting BedStudio online. Use a staged launch:

1. Landing page with brand, showroom, categories, trust, offer, and contact.
2. Core category pages.
3. Priority product pages.
4. Buying guides and SEO support pages.
5. Full product/catalogue expansion.

## Phase 1 - Launch Landing Page

Goal: make BedStudio credible immediately when people see signage, search the brand, or click a social/ad link.

Recommended sections:

1. Hero: BedStudio Newmarket, premium beds and mattresses.
2. Short positioning: NZ-made, customisable, showroom-led advice.
3. Shop by category: mattresses, bed bases, headboards, storage beds, adjustable beds.
4. Browse by size: single, king single, double, queen, king, super king, California king.
5. Browse by feel: plush/soft, medium, firm.
6. Browse by sleeper type: side, back, front, couple/split comfort.
7. Newmarket showroom block: 25B Broadway, Newmarket, Auckland 1023; phone 09 972 4142; hours/map/parking notes once confirmed.
8. Launch offer block: only after final wording is approved.
9. Delivery, warranty, and payment trust strip.
10. FAQ block.
11. Call/contact CTA.

## Navigation Recommendation

Keep the main menu simple:

- Mattresses
- Beds & Bases
- Headboards
- Storage Beds
- Adjustable Beds
- Newmarket Showroom
- Delivery & Support

Optional secondary navigation:

- Shop by Size
- Shop by Feel
- Finance Options
- Offers

## Category Page Standard

Each category page should include:

- clear H1
- short answer-first intro
- product grid
- buying guidance
- showroom advice block
- delivery and warranty notes
- internal links to related categories
- FAQs
- schema where suitable

## Product Page Standard

Each product page should include:

- product name rewritten for BedStudio
- 40-80 word premium summary
- who it is for
- size/feel options
- NZ-made/customisation notes where applicable
- warranty
- lead time
- delivery/assembly notes
- FAQs
- related products
- showroom CTA

Do not copy Budget Beds descriptions directly. Use the product export for facts, then rewrite for BedStudio positioning.
