# Scrap Mart Current Website Audit

Audited: 18 June 2026

Website: https://www.scrapmart.co.nz/

## Current Strengths

- Existing domain and indexed website.
- Clear current Auckland address: 11 Edinburgh Street, Onehunga.
- Phone number is visible: 09 941 7162.
- Current site mentions commercial and tradie collection Auckland-wide.
- Same-day payment and certified weighing are already part of the message.
- About page has a useful founder story for Ash Sampath.
- Current site mentions AMR membership.
- Current site has customer testimonials.
- Existing pages for household, commercial, tradie, metal scrap, used batteries, gallery, FAQs, reviews, and contact.

## Main Problems

1. Household scrap is too prominent.

The homepage and navigation give household scrap too much weight compared with the client's actual commercial/trade focus.

2. No clear live pricing system.

Competitors are using live or regularly updated price tables. Scrap Mart needs this for conversion and SEO.

3. Metal categories are too shallow.

The current "Metal Scrap" page is generic and does not properly target copper, aluminium, batteries, brass, stainless, motors, cables, radiators, air conditioners, or hot water cylinders.

4. Weak location architecture.

The current site is Auckland/Onehunga only. It needs a careful Christchurch expansion path without creating thin suburb pages.

5. Commercial collection and recurring pickup are underdeveloped.

The services exist, but the website does not give enough detail for factories, tradies, demolition, pickup, drop-off, or recurring collections. Bin services should not be included in the current structure because the client asked to remove them on 24 June 2026.

6. Conversion path is basic.

Forms and CTAs are generic. The new site should route people by intent: prices, drop-off, collection, commercial account, and Christchurch launch.

7. Proof is underused.

The current website has good raw material: testimonials, founder experience, certified weighing, same-day payment, Warren Metals history, AMR membership. These should be used more deliberately.

## Recommended Fixes

- Rebuild homepage around trade/commercial buyers, prices, high-value metals, and Auckland/Christchurch locations.
- Add structured price pages with weekly updates.
- Add "what we buy" metal pages with grade explanations and price tables.
- Add commercial service pages for pickup, factory scrap, industrial scrap, construction/demolition, and recurring collection.
- Keep household as a support page, not a top commercial priority.
- Build city-level location pages for Auckland and Christchurch first. Mention Onehunga, Penrose, Mangere, Manukau, East Tamaki, South Auckland, and Greenlane inside the Auckland page rather than launching thin suburb pages immediately.
- Add FAQ content around ID, payment, grading, price changes, pickup rules, and not accepted items.
- Add schema later: LocalBusiness, RecyclingCenter, Product/Offer where suitable, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList.
