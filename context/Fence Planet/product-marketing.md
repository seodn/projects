## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Customers can buy PVC fencing and gate products from retailers or cheaper suppliers, but many do not know how to install them properly, especially sliding gates with motors and site-specific requirements.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Supply-only sellers can make materials look cheap, but customers still need qualified installation.
- Customers often buy 10-20% more material than needed when they self-purchase.
- Retailer pricing can be high for some gate products, while still leaving the customer to solve installation.
- Builders and cheapest-price buyers may focus on supply cost without considering final installed quality.
- Wood, bamboo, and other alternatives require repainting, oiling, or ongoing maintenance over time.

**What it costs customers:** Extra material spend, installation mistakes, time coordinating trades, unclear final costs, long-term maintenance expense, poor gate operation, and lost opportunity to improve property frontage.

**Emotional tension:** Homeowners want the house to look better and feel secure, but they may be unsure whether PVC is worth the upfront cost, whether the installer is competent, and whether the final bill will grow beyond the first quote.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Supply plus installation in one service.
- Strong sliding-gate capability; the client called sliding gates the biggest winner.
- Ability to handle complicated gate jobs, including motor and electrical coordination.
- Transparent quoting: preliminary quote first, then final pricing based on actual installed posts, sections, and work completed.
- Customers do not need to buy unnecessary extra material.
- Licensed and compliant work under Australian rules and regulations.
- PVC positioned around lower total cost of ownership over a long period, not just upfront price.
- Two-year workmanship / defect-liability period was discussed as available for installation work.

**How we do it differently:** Fence Planet does not only sell "the chicken"; it sells the finished dish. The homeowner brings the problem of an unfinished frontage, fence, or gate, and Fence Planet helps decide the practical solution, supplies the material, installs it, handles motor requirements where relevant, and invoices after the job is accepted.

**Why that is better:** Customers get a working installed outcome instead of a pile of materials and a separate installation problem. They also reduce waste and get clearer final billing because they are charged for what was actually installed.

**Why customers choose Fence Planet:** Sliding-gate know-how, end-to-end responsibility, practical site advice, lower long-term maintenance, no hidden material overbuying, transparent final pricing, and licensed installation.
