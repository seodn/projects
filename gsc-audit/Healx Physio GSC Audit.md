### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** Google trusts your homepage for brand (`healx physio`) and broad local (`physiotherapy near me`), but it does *not* trust your service pages for specific local modifiers.
- **Action:** The `/services/physiotherapy` page currently reads like a textbook definition of physiotherapy. To gain Google's trust, it must read like a *local clinic page*. Add photos of the actual clinic interior and the specific physiotherapists who work there.

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Build these 3 Core Local Hubs next:**
