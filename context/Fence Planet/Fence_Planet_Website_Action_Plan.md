# Fence Planet Website Action Plan

**Audit date:** 2026-09-11  
**Website audited:** https://www.fenceplanet.com.au/  
**Context used:** `Fence Planet/.agents/product-marketing.md`  
**Purpose:** Page-by-page website update plan based on the live site and the Fence Planet client meeting context.

## Executive Summary

Fence Planet's website already covers PVC fencing, PVC gates, low maintenance, Sydney service coverage, free quotes, and basic trust messaging. The biggest gap is that the site does not yet express the client's strongest positioning:

- Fence Planet is strongest when the customer wants a complete installed result, not just cheap material.
- Sliding gates should be treated as a hero service and high-value lead driver.
- The business should explain its transparent quote model: preliminary quote first, final price based on what is actually installed.
- The site should teach total cost of ownership: PVC may cost more upfront, but saves on painting, oiling, rotting, and maintenance over years.
- Warranty and workmanship wording must be cleaned up because the site currently shows conflicting claims.
- Offers and quote CTAs should be more compelling, especially the agreed "spring special" / seasonal discount angle.

## Priority Order

1. **Fix trust and claim issues first:** warranty, pricing, years/projects/satisfaction stats, compliance wording, testimonials.
2. **Reposition homepage and PVC Gates page around sliding-gate installation and one-stop service.**
3. **Add missing quote/payment/process content:** free quote, 30% deposit, 70% after customer acceptance, bank transfer, site-specific pricing.
4. **Create new lead pages for high-intent searches:** PVC sliding gates Sydney, automated sliding gates Sydney, PVC fence installation Sydney, PVC fencing cost Sydney, PVC vs timber fencing.
5. **Improve conversion paths:** quote forms, offer banners, project captions, clearer service dropdowns, stronger CTAs.

## Global Site Changes

### Replace Generic Positioning Across Site

**Current issue:** The site repeatedly says generic things like "reliable PVC fencing supply and installation" and "Hampton PVC fencing contractors." This is serviceable SEO copy, but it does not reflect the client's actual competitive edge.

**Replace with core positioning:**

> PVC fencing and sliding gates, supplied and installed by people who know how to make them work.

Use this message across hero sections, footer intro, service intros, and quote CTAs.

### Make Sliding Gates a Primary Service

**Current issue:** Sliding gates are present, but they are not treated as the biggest opportunity. The client clearly identified sliding gates as the "biggest winner."

**Change needed:**

- Add "PVC Sliding Gates" to the main navigation as its own page.
- Add a homepage section specifically for sliding gates.
- Add a homepage service card for "PVC Sliding Gate Installation" separate from general "PVC Gate Installation."
- Add quote-form service options for:
  - PVC fencing installation
  - PVC sliding gate installation
  - Automated gate installation
  - PVC swing gate installation
  - Supply only
  - Repair / existing fence replacement

### Add Transparent Quote and No-Waste Messaging

**Current issue:** The website talks about free quotes but does not explain the client's strongest pricing story.

**Add site-wide message:**

> We start with a clear preliminary quote, then finalise your price based on what is actually installed. That means you are not left paying for unnecessary extra posts, sections, or materials.

**Where to add:**

- Homepage pricing/process section
- Services page
- PVC Fencing page pricing section
- PVC Gates page pricing section
- FAQ page
- Contact page above the form

### Fix Warranty and Workmanship Claims

**Current issue:** The older indexed version mentions a 15-year warranty; current Terms say installation work is guaranteed for 1 year; the client meeting discussed product warranty separately from a 2-year installation workmanship / defect-liability period. This must be reconciled before publishing stronger warranty copy.

**Action:**

- Confirm current product warranty period and exact manufacturer coverage.
- Confirm whether Fence Planet's workmanship / defect-liability period is 1 year or 2 years.
- Update every page to use the same wording.

**Recommended public wording after confirmation:**

> Product warranties are covered by the manufacturer warranty for the selected PVC product. Fence Planet also stands behind our installation workmanship under the agreed workmanship period provided with your invoice.

If 2 years is confirmed:

> Our installation workmanship is covered for 2 years, with warranty details provided on your final invoice.

### Verify or Remove Big Stats

**Current issue:** Homepage claims "15+ Years Experience," "1000+ Projects," and "98% Satisfaction." Another site connected to the same business uses "500+ reviews" and "99% defect-free delivery rate." These are strong claims but not supported in the meeting context.

**Action:**

  - "Sydney and surrounds"
  - "Free site quotes"
  - "Supply and installation"

### Add Seasonal Offer Banner

**Current issue:** The client approved using a seasonal offer idea, but no clear promotion is visible.

**Add banner site-wide:**

**Important:** Do not mention 10%, 15%, or 20% unless the client confirms the exact discount.

### Add Local Trust and Compliance Language

**Current issue:** The site says professional/certified/compliant in places, but it does not clearly connect that to Australian rules, licensing, gate automation, and electrical work.

**Add wording:**

> Fence Planet follows Australian rules, regulations, and compliance requirements for fencing and gate installation. For automated gates, we coordinate the work needed for safe, compliant operation.
