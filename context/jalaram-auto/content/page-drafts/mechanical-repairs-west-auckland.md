## Common Problems
### What signs mean your car needs a mechanic?

Book a check if you notice:

- Squealing, grinding or vibration while braking.
- Clunks, knocks or uneven handling.
- A slipping clutch or trouble changing gears.
- Warning lights on the dash.
- Leaks, overheating or engine noise.
- A WOF issue that needs repair.

Small symptoms can turn into bigger repairs if they are left too long.
