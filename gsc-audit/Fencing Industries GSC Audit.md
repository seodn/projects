## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Push your high-value service pages from Page 3 to Page 1.
- **Hidden Demand:** Security Fencing and Commercial Fencing.
- **Action:** Your internal pages lack local semantic trust. Update the H1 on the `/security-fencing` page from just "Security Fencing" to "Commercial Security Fencing Contractors Christchurch". 

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Homepage**
- **Title Tag:** `Fencing Industries | Commercial & Security Fencing Christchurch`
- **Meta Description:** `Christchurch's leading commercial fencing contractors. We specialize in high-security fencing, industrial gates, and timber post & rail. Contact us today.`

**2. Security Fencing Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Security Fencing Christchurch | Industrial Fencing Contractors`
- **Meta Description:** `Protect your commercial property with heavy-duty security fencing. From chainlink to high-security mesh systems, we install across Christchurch and Canterbury.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** In the B2B commercial sector, Google trusts Case Studies, corporate logos (clients you've worked with), and massive galleries.
- **Action:** Take 3 projects from your `/projects` page and embed them directly onto the `/products-services/security-fencing` page as "Recent Christchurch Installations". 

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Content Gap Attack):**
To capture top-of-funnel B2B traffic and facility managers looking for solutions:
1. *A Facility Manager's Guide to Commercial Security Fencing in NZ*
2. *Chainlink vs Mesh Security Fencing: Which is Best for Your Warehouse?*
3. *Health & Safety Compliance for Industrial Fencing and Gates in NZ*
4. *How to Choose the Right Timber Post & Rail Fencing for Rural Estates*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** Homepage (`/`).
- **Recipient Pages:** `/products-services/security-fencing` and `/sectors/commercial`.
- **Exact Implementation:** The homepage needs a highly visible "Our Expertise" section that links directly to the Security Fencing page using the exact anchor text `Security Fencing Christchurch`.
