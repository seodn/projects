# Diamonds For Everyone New Zealand Website and SEO Audit Findings

**Website:** https://diamondsforeveryone.co.nz/  
**Related Australian website:** https://diamondsforeveryone.com.au/  
**Audit date:** 1 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Internal audit reference and consultation planning  

## Scope and evidence

This audit consolidates the public website review, the earlier client audit discussion, and the findings documented by Sagar Bhola on 30 September 2026.

The review covered 75 HTML URLs, sitemap and crawl configuration, representative desktop and mobile journeys, and the appointment calendar. No enquiry, booking, checkout or order was submitted. Form delivery, checkout completion and conversion tracking still require controlled validation with account access.

The New Zealand site has a credible commercial base: a broad product range, customisation, New Zealand pricing and consultation options. The priority is to correct the trust, localisation, conversion, content and technical issues that weaken the path from search visit to qualified enquiry.

## Sagar audit findings

These verified issues are listed first because they were documented in Sagar's email and attached audit.

### 1. Incorrect contact email link

- The Contact page displays `info@diamondsforeveryone.co.nz`.
- The underlying email link targets `info@diamondsforeveryone.com.nz`.
- The shared Email support button opens Gmail specifically instead of the visitor's preferred email application.
- Correct the destination, use a standard mail link and validate enquiry delivery.

### 2. Homepage banner calls to action

- The visible “View Collection” and “Shop Now” calls to action are embedded in promotional imagery.
- The slideshow does not provide corresponding accessible text buttons.
- Clicking the tested View Collection area did not navigate.
- Replace artwork-only calls to action with real links and visible text headlines.

### 3. Empty collection routes

- Fourteen of the 37 checked collection routes display zero products.
- Examples include Oval Rings, Wedding Rings, Fine Jewellery, Emerald Rings and Nature Inspired.
- Some empty routes appear in the sitemap, navigation or homepage sections.
- Populate commercially useful collections or consolidate and redirect them to relevant working pages.

### 4. Unfinished public pages

- The Sustainability page contains unfinished or template content.
- Older appointment and ring-builder pages remain publicly accessible with incomplete material.
- The 1ct promotional page advertises a $999 offer alongside example products and placeholder prices.
- The Contact page includes internal-sounding copy about building trust instead of customer-facing information.
- Complete, consolidate, redirect or remove these pages from indexable customer journeys.

### 5. Conflicting return promises

- Prominent site badges state “30 Day Return”.
- The detailed return policy excludes change-of-mind returns.
- Confirm the real policy and make homepage, product, FAQ and policy wording consistent.

### 6. Mixed New Zealand and Australian messaging

- The footer lists an Auckland address.
- The homepage, About page and FAQs include Brisbane or Australian references.
- Some content links send New Zealand visitors to the Australian store.
- Clearly explain the New Zealand operation, appointment location, fulfilment process and after-sales arrangements.
- Correct unintended cross-market links and references.

### 7. Price, deposit and delivery clarity

- Setting-only and complete-ring pricing labels are not consistently applied.
- Product pages mention a $500 deposit.
- The payment page describes 50% deposits and 20% layby terms.
- Production is described as three to four weeks, with a further one to five business days for New Zealand shipping.
- Consolidate these rules and place the relevant explanation near the buying controls.

### 8. Mobile interruptions and trust

- A newsletter popup, separate help popup and several floating contact controls interrupt browsing.
- The displayed Google rating needs a direct link to its genuine source and a current review count.
- Team, certification, craftsmanship and customer evidence need stronger presentation.
- Consolidate mobile overlays and strengthen proof close to enquiry and purchase decisions.

### 9. Homepage performance

- A single Google PageSpeed Insights run returned 56 out of 100 on mobile and 58 out of 100 on desktop.
- Largest Contentful Paint was 12.4 seconds on simulated mobile and 1.5 seconds on desktop.
- No real-user field data was available in the reviewed report.
- Review render-blocking resources, Shopify apps and scripts, fonts, image delivery and above-the-fold media.
- Retest after implementation and monitor real-user Core Web Vitals when data becomes available.

### 10. Inconsistent on-page SEO

- The homepage title contains only the brand name.
- The homepage lacks a meta description.
- Thirteen checked collection routes lack meta descriptions.
- Several commercially relevant ring-style pages need stronger titles, headings and visible New Zealand buying context.

### 11. Thin collection guidance

- Priority collection pages need useful information about styles, metals, total pricing, sizing, delivery and customisation.
- Relevant FAQs and intentional internal links are limited.
- Improve existing commercial pages before producing a large volume of general blog content.

### 12. Duplicate routes and crawl configuration

- Two men's wedding-band collections contain the same 15 products under separate URLs.
- The robots.txt file contains joined-together instructions that require repair and validation.
- These findings do not by themselves prove a Google penalty or site-wide indexing block.
- Confirm the preferred route, canonical and redirect approach before implementation.

### 13. New Zealand and Australia regional setup

- No hreflang tags were found in the sampled New Zealand HTML.
- Equivalent pages across the New Zealand and Australian stores require deliberate regional mapping.
- New Zealand pages need genuinely local service, policy, pricing and fulfilment information.
- Do not automatically point New Zealand canonical tags to the Australian website.

### 14. Measurement and search growth

- Some New Zealand pages appear in public search.
- Useful foundations are present, including product structured data and relevant metadata on several categories.
- Actual organic traffic, ranking trends, qualified enquiries, sales and revenue require Search Console, GA4, Shopify and lead data.
- A Lighthouse SEO score is a limited technical checklist and should not be presented as an overall SEO performance rating.

## Broader audit findings

### New Zealand localisation and factual accuracy

- Remove remaining Australian and Brisbane references from New Zealand customer journeys unless the relationship is intentionally explained.
- Confirm whether customers can visit the Auckland address and how appointments operate.
- Define the relationship between the New Zealand business and the Brisbane operation.
- Use New Zealand spelling, currency, delivery expectations, contact details and consumer-policy language consistently.
- Review image filenames and alt text that still contain Australian market references.

### Enquiry journey and conversion reliability

- Validate every consultation, email, telephone, WhatsApp and product-to-enquiry path.
- Track meaningful actions separately from superficial contact-button clicks.
- Confirm that forms deliver to the correct team and that submissions receive an appropriate confirmation.
- Give visitors a clear next action on product, collection, custom jewellery, appointment and educational pages.
- Reduce overlapping mobile controls and avoid interrupting visitors before they have engaged with the page.

### Buying information and ecommerce messaging

- Standardise setting-only, diamond-inclusive and complete-ring terminology.
- Explain deposits, layby, payment schedules, production times and delivery close to the main buying controls.
- Align returns, resizing, warranty, price-match and shipping information across templates.
- Review promotional prices and discount claims for accuracy and expiry handling.
- Distinguish online purchase journeys from consultation-led or made-to-order journeys.

### Technical SEO and index control

- Repair and validate robots.txt formatting.
- Review XML sitemaps for empty, incomplete, legacy and duplicate routes.
- Confirm canonical tags on collections, products, filtered routes and cross-market equivalents.
- Create a redirect plan for retired or consolidated pages.
- Validate structured data for Product, Breadcrumb, Organisation and relevant local business information.
- Monitor indexing, exclusions and crawl anomalies in Google Search Console.
- Improve mobile performance by template rather than treating the homepage as the only performance problem.

### Site architecture and internal linking

- Build a clear hierarchy across engagement rings, wedding bands, fine jewellery, diamonds, gemstones, custom jewellery and education.
- Connect educational pages to appropriate collection and product pages.
- Connect diamond-shape and carat guidance to the relevant commercial destinations.
- Link collections to useful supporting guides, FAQs, consultations and custom-design options.
- Use descriptive anchor text and avoid creating multiple pages for the same intent.
- Identify orphaned pages and remove links to empty or unfinished destinations.

### Collection page SEO

- Prioritise collections using New Zealand demand, commercial value, margin and production capacity.
- Assign one primary search intent to each target collection URL.
- Add unique titles, meta descriptions, headings and introductory guidance.
- Cover styles, metals, stones, price composition, customisation, sizing, production and delivery.
- Add relevant FAQs and links to deeper educational resources.
- Avoid near-duplicate category text across the New Zealand and Australian sites.

### Product page SEO and conversion

- Standardise product naming and remove unnecessary Australian market references.
- Clarify exactly what the displayed price includes.
- Present setting, stone, metal, size, production, shipping and after-sales information consistently.
- Add credible craftsmanship, certification and customisation evidence.
- Strengthen consultation and enquiry options for customers who are not ready to buy online.
- Validate product schema, availability, pricing and variant information.

### Content quality and keyword mapping

- Create a keyword-to-URL map before publishing new content.
- Strengthen commercial pages before expanding general informational publishing.
- Build supporting content around diamond choices, ring styles, pricing, sizing, custom design, delivery and New Zealand buying questions.
- Use clear expert attribution and first-hand business evidence.
- Consolidate content that targets the same intent.
- Keep New Zealand pages distinct instead of copying Australian content with place-name substitutions.

### Trust, authority and E-E-A-T

- Explain who operates the New Zealand business and who provides consultations, design guidance and after-sales support.
- Add verifiable team, experience, certification and craftsmanship evidence.
- Show genuine review sources and keep ratings and review totals current.
- Strengthen policy clarity and ensure trust badges match the detailed terms.
- Add authentic New Zealand customer, appointment and fulfilment evidence where available.
- Earn relevant local and industry mentions rather than relying only on self-published claims.

### Trustpilot and review strategy

- Review how the Australian business uses Trustpilot and other review sources.
- Confirm whether the New Zealand profile should be separate or connected under the platform's rules.
- Request reviews after a verified completed purchase or consultation milestone.
- Use a consistent invitation and response process without scripting sentiment or using prohibited incentives.
- Display review provenance, rating and count clearly.
- Track review volume, average rating, response rate and recurring customer themes.

### Australia learnings without duplication

- Reuse proven audience insights, objections, review processes and paid creative themes from Australia.
- Compare Australian and New Zealand query demand before selecting priorities.
- Adapt offers, policies, logistics, pricing and service evidence for New Zealand.
- Avoid copying Australian page text, metadata, testimonials or location claims.
- Use hreflang only after equivalent pages and canonical rules have been mapped correctly.

### Conversion rate optimisation

- Establish the preferred conversion for each template: purchase, consultation, custom-design enquiry, call or email.
- Improve calls to action, mobile navigation and form usability.
- Place buying reassurance beside the action it supports.
- Test consultation positioning, price clarity, delivery messaging and trust proof.
- Review behaviour by device, landing page and new versus returning visitor.
- Prioritise tests with enough traffic and clear success criteria.

### Accessibility and user experience

- Replace text embedded in promotional images with accessible HTML text and functional links.
- Review heading order and page hierarchy.
- Improve image alt text and remove inaccurate market references.
- Check keyboard navigation, focus states, colour contrast and form labels.
- Ensure popups and floating controls can be dismissed and do not obstruct content.
- Test representative journeys across common mobile breakpoints.

### Tracking and reporting

- Define the funnel from organic landing page to enquiry, qualified lead, booked consultation, sale and revenue.
- Track calls, email enquiries, WhatsApp, consultation bookings, contact forms and purchases separately.
- Capture lead source, landing page, enquiry type, outcome and loss reason where possible.
- Reconcile analytics with Shopify, appointment and lead-management records.
- Report qualified enquiry and consultation outcomes alongside rankings and traffic.
- Establish a baseline before major changes so impact can be evaluated accurately.

## Recommended consulting programme

### Days 1 to 14: validate and prioritise

- Confirm business rules, conversion definitions and New Zealand operating details.
- Review Search Console, GA4, Shopify, Google Business Profile, Merchant Center and advertising data.
- Analyse the outcome of the current 13 to 14 enquiries.
- Convert the audit into a shared backlog with priority, owner and acceptance criteria.
- Approve the first implementation release.

### Days 15 to 45: fix the foundations

- Correct enquiry, contact, policy, localisation and unfinished-content issues.
- Repair crawl configuration, duplicate routes and technical template problems.
- Improve homepage and priority-template performance.
- Establish reliable event and lead tracking.
- Update priority titles, descriptions and headings.

### Days 46 to 90: strengthen commercial pages

- Complete the keyword-to-URL map.
- Upgrade priority collection and product templates.
- Implement the internal-linking plan.
- Publish high-value New Zealand buying guidance and FAQs.
- Improve consultation and custom-design conversion paths.

### Days 91 to 120: build authority and scale

- Launch the approved Trustpilot and review process.
- Add team, certification, consultation and craftsmanship evidence.
- Develop relevant New Zealand partnerships, mentions and digital PR opportunities.
- Refresh pages using Search Console and conversion data.
- Scale the page and content patterns that produce qualified demand.

## Ownership model

### Consultant

- Diagnose and prioritise.
- Create technical, content and conversion briefs.
- Translate useful Australian learnings for the New Zealand market.
- Review implementation and independently QA releases.
- Lead monthly performance and decision reviews.

### Internal SEO team

- Maintain keyword and URL mapping.
- Prepare metadata, collection copy, FAQs and supporting content.
- Implement internal linking.
- Monitor rankings, search visibility, indexation and organic landing-page performance.
- Document completed work and unresolved blockers.

### Developer team

- Implement template, navigation, performance and technical fixes.
- Correct crawl, canonical, redirect, hreflang and structured-data configuration.
- Implement tracking requirements.
- Support controlled release, rollback and QA processes.

## Core KPIs

- Qualified organic enquiries.
- Consultation booking rate.
- Enquiry-to-sale rate and organic-assisted revenue.
- Priority collection and product page clicks and visibility.
- Conversion rate by landing-page template and device.
- Index coverage and crawl health.
- Core Web Vitals by template.
- Review volume, rating and response rate.
- Lead response time and recorded loss reasons.

## Reporting cadence

- **Weekly:** backlog, ownership, blockers and release status.
- **Fortnightly:** implementation QA and acceptance decisions.
- **Monthly:** search, enquiry, lead-quality and revenue review with the next priority decisions.
- **End of each roadmap phase:** steering review and approval of the next phase.

## Important limitations

- PageSpeed results cited above are single-run laboratory measurements and should be retested.
- Public review cannot confirm lead delivery, checkout completion, account-level indexation or revenue attribution.
- Regional tags and canonicals should not be changed until equivalent New Zealand and Australian pages have been mapped.
- Legal and consumer-policy wording should be confirmed by the business and, where appropriate, its legal adviser.
