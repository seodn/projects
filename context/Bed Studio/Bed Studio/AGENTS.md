## Writing Rules

- Use New Zealand/British English.
- Keep the tone premium but practical.
- Separate confirmed facts from recommendations.
- Do not invent address details, launch dates, offers, discounts, product availability, warranty terms, or review claims.
- Do not copy Budget Beds product descriptions directly onto BedStudio.
- Rewrite shared products with BedStudio-specific positioning, showroom advice, FAQs, delivery notes, and premium context.
- Avoid unsupported health claims. For adjustable beds and comfort products, use careful wording such as "comfort", "support", "ease of use", and "personal preference" unless a supplier provides approved claim wording.
