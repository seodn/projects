## Product Overview

**One-liner — proposed:** PRA Developments creates quality new-build homes in carefully selected Christchurch locations, with clear information and personal support throughout the buying journey.

**What it does — meeting:** PRA develops and sells new homes, including townhouses and apartments. Many projects sell off plan. Show homes demonstrate quality and finishes; detailed brochures explain the selected development. PRA works with external specialists and sales agents.

**Category:** Residential property development; Christchurch new-build homes and townhouses for sale.

**Product type:** High-consideration residential property purchase.

**Business model:** Property sales. Prices, available units, specifications, inclusions, sale conditions, and completion dates are development-specific. No approved general price range was supplied. PRA does not provide home loans; lending is arranged separately.

**Geography — meeting:** Christchurch is the NZ focus. Paiman said Perth operations began in 2023 and he had moved there full-time by the meeting. Perth inventory, local terms, and website have not been audited for this pack.

## Target Audience

PRA serves household and individual buyers, not a conventional B2B account profile. First-home buyers and investors are the primary audiences; downsizers are secondary. The approximate 80% / 20% split was a meeting estimate, not a verified reporting metric.

| Buyer | Job to be done | Information needed | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-home buyer | Understand and buy a suitable new home confidently | Buying process, plans, specifications, finance process, off-plan explanation, aftercare | Brochure; question; show-home visit |
| Investor | Assess a property against investment criteria | Price, title, council rates, specifications, rental appraisal, supported yield assumptions | Information pack and sales conversation |
| Downsizer | Move to a more manageable home in a convenient location | Layout, maintenance, space, amenities, everyday suitability | Floor plan and viewing |

Motivations above are proposed interpretations of the interview, not verbatim customer research. Do not assume universal accessibility or suitability for older buyers without inspecting the layout. Decision-makers are buyers/households; family, lenders, and professional advisers may influence the decision. No formal stakeholder study was supplied.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Meeting:** Buyers ask about specifications, property/title type, rates, and rental returns. First-home buyers may need help with unfamiliar steps, appliance operation, finishes, and maintenance. Off-plan property is harder to evaluate because it is not yet built.

**Proposed emotional needs:** Confidence in quality and the developer, clarity on inclusions, and reassurance that questions will be answered after purchase.

**Potential friction:** Scattered information, mismatched walkthrough addresses, unsupported general claims, and unclear aftercare. The walkthrough mismatch was discussed in the meeting; current interactive functionality has not been tested.

**Cost:** More clarification time and uncertainty. No quantified time-saving, cost-saving, or lost-sales evidence was supplied.

## Differentiation

Retain **PRA standard**. Do not create a new acronym. In the interview it covers the product, location, and service. It is a brand promise, not a formal certification.

| Pillar | Message direction | Supporting evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Home quality | Thoughtful design, materials, finishes, workmanship | Project specifications, plans, photographs, documented checks |

## Objections & Fit

These are proposed responses based on meeting topics, not ranked sales-objection research.

| Buyer question | Response direction |
|---|---|
| How do I judge an off-plan home? | Relevant plans/specifications, show-home visit, clearly labelled property-specific or representative media |
| What exactly am I buying? | Latest brochure; clarify inclusions, title, layout, price, and contract questions with sales |
| What deposit will I need? | Buyer and lender circumstances matter; distinguish lending deposit/equity from contractual deposit; verify local wording |
| What if something needs attention? | Explain written defect-reporting process and separate product warranties, without unrestricted coverage promises |

**Proposed poor fit:** Buyers seeking PRA-provided loans, guaranteed returns, or unavailable features. No demographic exclusions or affordability thresholds are established.

## Switching Dynamics

Proposed hypotheses to validate with customers:
- **Push:** Current home no longer suits; maintenance burden; desire to move beyond renting.
- **Pull:** Suitable new home, convenience, clear information, tangible quality, responsive contact.
- **Habit:** Familiarity with current home or postponing a major purchase.
- **Anxiety:** Off-plan uncertainty, finance, construction quality, timing, aftercare.

## Customer Language

No verified verbatim buyer interviews were supplied. Reported buyer questions are team recollections, not testimonial quotes. Founder/team phrases include “PRA standard,” “serving the community,” and “premium product at affordable price.” Do not attribute these to customers.

**Use:** New-build homes, townhouses, carefully selected locations, quality finishes, detailed property information, personal support, family-owned; low-maintenance where supported.

**Avoid:** “Silly questions,” guaranteed growth/returns, blanket deposit promises, invented credentials, unsupported best/biggest rankings, awkward repeated keywords, and empty superlatives.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PRA standard | Brand promise about home quality, location, service |
| Off plan | Purchase using agreed plans/specifications before completion |
| Show home | Completed quality example; not automatically the purchased unit |
| Representative walkthrough | Typical layout/completed example; label actual address and differences |
| Gross rental yield | Rental-income measure, not capital appreciation or guaranteed profit |
| Defect coverage | Governed by confirmed terms; distinct from appliance warranties |
