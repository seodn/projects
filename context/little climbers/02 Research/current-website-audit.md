# Current Website Audit

Website reviewed: https://littleclimbers.co.nz/

Date reviewed: 3 July 2026

## Summary

The current website has the right basic foundation: four locations, book-a-visit CTAs, curriculum, transition, contact details, testimonials, and visual childcare imagery.

The main issue is that the copy is too generic, repeated, and sometimes inconsistent with the transcript. For the redesign, the site needs to become more precise, centre-specific, and parent-question-led.

## Current Strengths

- Four main locations are visible in the navigation and homepage.
- Location pages exist for East Tamaki, Mangere, Otara, and Pukekohe.
- Contact details are repeated in the footer and contact page.
- Phone and email are clear.
- Book a Visit / Apply Now CTAs exist.
- Curriculum and transition pages exist.
- Parent testimonials exist.
- Location pages include some relevant keywords.
- The site already talks about play-based learning, cultural diversity, creativity, health and wellbeing.

## Current Issues

### 1. "Best childcare" overuse

The website repeatedly uses "Best Daycare in Auckland" and similar phrases.

Why it matters:

- The client specifically preferred "quality childcare" because "best" feels self-claimed.
- "Best" is hard to prove without external awards/rankings.
- It can sound less credible than a specific, parent-focused value proposition.

Recommended fix:

- Replace broad "best" claims with specific proof-led language:
  - quality childcare
  - small local centres
  - individual learning plans
  - pickup/drop-off at selected centres
  - support with 20 Hours ECE, WINZ and FamilyBoost
  - transition-to-school support

### 2. Generic repeated location copy

The location pages are too similar. They use similar blocks for play-based learning, cultural diversity, creativity, health and wellbeing.

Why it matters:

- Google and AI search need unique centre-specific detail.
- Parents need practical information for the centre they are considering.
- Thin or repeated location copy weakens local SEO.

Recommended fix:

- Rebuild each location page around transcript details:
  - Mangere: Kingsford Primary, library/story time, Tongan/Pacific community, no babies, morning/afternoon tea, transport.
  - Otara: Pasifika community, free transport, food support, all age stages, library visits.
  - Dannemora/East Tamaki: Willowbank Primary, vegetarian meals, baby/toddler/preschool rooms, no transport, Botany/Flat Bush.
  - Pukekohe: 2-6 years, transport, food, Pokeno/Drury, infant care only if confirmed.

### 3. Factual inconsistencies

Known conflicts:

- Otara hours: current site says 7:00am-6:00pm; transcript says 7:00am-5:00pm.
- Pukekohe hours: current site says 7:00am-6:00pm; transcript says 7:30am-5:30pm.
- Homepage says flexible hours from 7:00am to 8:00pm, which does not match current centre hours.
- Homepage says "0-6" broadly, but Mangere and Pukekohe are not currently all-age centres according to the transcript.
- Contact form includes Mount Eden/Balmoral, which was not part of the main centre discussion.
- Footer mixes home-based care into the main website, although transcript says home-based has a separate website.

Recommended fix:

- Create a verified location data table.
- Do not publish the redesign until each centre's hours, ages, meals and transport are confirmed.
- Remove or explain Mount Eden/Balmoral.
- Separate home-based from centre-based pages.

### 4. Weak parent decision content

The current site does not go deep enough into the questions parents actually ask.

Missing or weak topics:

- how fees work
- 20 Hours ECE
- WINZ subsidy
- FamilyBoost
- transport by centre
- meals by centre
- transition visits
- what to do if a child cries at drop-off
- what age to start childcare
- what to look for on a centre tour
- school readiness
- separation anxiety
- teacher-child ratios, carefully worded
- allergy support
- nappies
- casual care

Recommended fix:

- Add a proper FAQ and guide system.
- Add answer-first sections on location pages.
- Add dedicated guides for cost, subsidies, settling, and choosing childcare.

### 5. Local SEO underuse

The current site has location pages, but not enough useful local signals.

Missing local signals:

- nearby schools
- parks/libraries
- local suburbs children come from
- community activities
- centre-specific services
- centre-specific reviews
- centre-specific photos
- centre-specific schema

Recommended fix:

- Add "nearby suburbs" and local context sections.
- Add internal links between centre pages and relevant guides.
- Add LocalBusiness / ChildCare structured data for each centre.

### 6. Proof is not organised by centre

Testimonials are present, but they are not strongly tied to each location.

Recommended fix:

- Collect and organise reviews by centre.
- Add centre-specific reviews to each location page.
- Add photo proof by centre.
- Add approved teacher/team proof by centre.

### 7. Design and messaging mismatch

The design direction discussed with the client is pastel, playful, warm, and polished.

Current issues:

- bright red elements may conflict with client preference
- text can feel overly promotional
- some placeholder/odd text remains, such as "crazy about toys", "kids playground", "many cool ideas", "colour and fun"
- Qode copyright remains in footer

Recommended fix:

- Shift to softer pastel palette.
- Clean footer.
- Use calmer, clearer parent-first copy.
- Avoid large blocks of generic text.

## Recommended Website Fix Priority
