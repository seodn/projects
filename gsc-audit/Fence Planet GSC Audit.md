## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Fix the massive structural issue preventing you from ranking on Page 1.
- **Hidden Demand:** Specific PVC styles (picket, semi-privacy, full privacy, pool fencing).
- **Action:** **CRITICAL ARCHITECTURE PIVOT.** You must delete the generic `/services` page concept and build a dropdown menu with dedicated landing pages: `/pvc-privacy-fencing/`, `/pvc-picket-fencing/`, and `/pvc-pool-fencing/`. 

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Homepage (Targeting the Sydney Win)**
- **Title Tag:** `PVC Fencing Sydney | Premium PVC Fence Suppliers & Installers`
- **Meta Description:** `Upgrade your home with durable, low-maintenance PVC fencing in Sydney. We supply and install premium PVC picket, privacy, and pool fences. Get a free quote!`

**2. New PVC Privacy Fencing Page (Once Built)**
- **Title Tag:** `PVC Privacy Fencing Panels Sydney | Fence Planet`
- **Meta Description:** `Looking for complete privacy? Our solid PVC fencing panels are weather-resistant, stylish, and perfect for Sydney homes. Browse our range today.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** In construction and trades, Google trusts before/after galleries and localized service areas.
- **Action:** Move your best `/gallery` photos directly onto the homepage and the new service pages. Don't make users click away to see your work.

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Content Gap Attack):**
To capture top-of-funnel traffic and convince homeowners that PVC is better than timber or Colorbond:
1. *PVC Fencing vs Timber: Which is the Best Investment for Sydney Homes?*
2. *Is PVC Pool Fencing Compliant in NSW? A Homeowner's Guide*
3. *How to Clean and Maintain Your White PVC Fence in 10 Minutes*
4. *The Cost of PVC Fencing in Sydney: 2024 Pricing Guide*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** Homepage (`/`).
- **Recipient Pages:** The new service pages (once built).
- **Exact Implementation:** The homepage needs a "Our Fencing Styles" grid. Each box in the grid must link to the new specific pages using exact match anchors like `PVC Picket Fencing` and `PVC Privacy Fencing`.
