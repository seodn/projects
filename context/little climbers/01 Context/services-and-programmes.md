# Services And Programmes

## Care Model

Little Climbers provides centre-based early childhood care and education. The model is:

- small centre sizes
- relationship-led parent communication
- child-specific learning and development plans
- play-based learning
- community-aware curriculum
- practical support for families through transport, meals, transition visits, and subsidy guidance

The client wants the new website to show the real substance behind the service, not generic childcare phrases.

Transcript and current site both support a transition-visit message.

Use careful wording:

- Little Climbers encourages transition visits before a child starts.
- The transition visits help the child get familiar with teachers, routines, the environment, and separation from parents.

## Parent Journey

Recommended public journey:

1. Parent enquires through phone, form, email, or Facebook.
2. Centre encourages the parent to visit.
3. Staff discuss the child's age, needs, hours, meals, transport, allergies, and family situation.
4. Staff explain fees, current offers if available, 20 Hours ECE, WINZ, and FamilyBoost support.
5. Parent receives enrolment forms.
6. Child completes transition visits.
7. Parent receives updates through face-to-face communication, email, and the online portal.
