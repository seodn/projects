## Strategic Summary

Scrap Mart needs a commercial-first website that makes three things obvious:

1. They buy scrap metal and pay fast.
2. They show weekly prices by metal grade.
3. They have two branch locations: Onehunga in Auckland and Hillsborough in Christchurch.

The new website should shift away from the current household-led structure and build around the search intents that create real enquiries: prices, copper, cables, batteries, aluminium, trade/commercial scrap, pickup, recurring collection, drop-off, and local yard searches.

Auckland and Christchurch should receive equal commercial and SEO weight. Auckland has the existing online footprint, but the live website should present Scrap Mart as a two-branch business from day one.

## Client-Supplied Keyword Assessment

The keyword attachment from Dharmesh does not require a major website restructure. It reinforces the existing city, price, metal, service, and resource pages.

Use these terms as supporting wording inside existing pages:

- Auckland and Christchurch city terms on the two location pages.
- Price, cash, and "today" terms on price pages.
- Copper, cable, aluminium, battery, brass, stainless, steel, lead, motor, radiator, air conditioner, hot water cylinder, zinc, and e-waste terms on matching What We Buy pages.
- Collection, pickup, drop-off, commercial, factory, industrial, construction, and recurring-contract terms on service pages.

Do not add these as pages:

- Car Bodies Scrap
- Car Engines Scrap
- Scrap Metal and Machinery
- Scrap Metal Bin Hire

Use with care:

- Used automotive battery price: only Auckland sells used batteries. Both branches buy scrap batteries.
- Public Free drop off metal scrap: use the cleaner wording "Free scrap metal drop-off for household appliances."

## Keyword Ownership

- Homepage owns Scrap Mart brand, broad scrap metal buyer category positioning, and two-branch trust.
- Price hub owns broad pricing intent.
- Local price pages own city-specific price intent.
- Metal pages own material/grade intent.
- Service pages own commercial workflow intent.
- Location pages own nearby yard/local buyer intent.
- Guides support featured snippets, AI-search visibility, and internal links back to money pages.
