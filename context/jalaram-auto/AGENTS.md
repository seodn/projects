# Jalaram Auto Client Workspace Instructions

This workspace is for the Jalaram Auto website rebuild and SEO project.

Jalaram Auto is an automotive repair workshop in Glen Eden, West Auckland. The new website plan should improve Google visibility for high-intent mechanic, WOF, hybrid battery, car servicing, and tyre searches around Glen Eden and nearby West Auckland suburbs.

## Client Context

- Public brand: Jalaram Auto.
- Legal/company name found in public company records: Jalaram Auto Limited.
- Confirmed public address from MTA listing and company records: 287C West Coast Road, Glen Eden, Auckland 0602.
- Confirmed public phone from MTA listing: 022 122 1822.
- Public MTA listing identifies Jalaram Auto as a Repairer.
- Meeting context identifies Chirag Thakkar as the owner/founder and the key trust differentiator.
- Client says reviews are genuine and should be treated as a major local trust asset.

## Website And SEO Priorities

- Lead with local search intent: mechanic Glen Eden, WOF Glen Eden, car service Glen Eden, hybrid mechanic near me, hybrid battery repair, and tyre puncture repair.
- Make hybrid battery repair and hybrid battery reconditioning a primary differentiator. Meeting context says this is a standout service in the local area.
- Build a service architecture that separates hybrid, WOF, servicing, mechanical repairs, electrical/air conditioning, and tyres.
- Treat minor denting/panel beating as a low-priority or excluded service unless the client reconfirms it, because the meeting indicated it is not profitable and only tiny jobs are done.
- Do not claim wheel alignment as an offered service. The client said alignment is not done.
- Use founder-led trust, MTA approval, genuine reviews, before/after repair photos, transparent explanations, and quality oil/parts as proof themes.
- Use the client-requested menu grouping unless later superseded: Home, Services, Tyre, Battery, WINZ, Hybrid, About, Contact, Gallery, FAQ.
- Treat MTA approval, Afterpay, guarantees, WINZ support, WOF availability, and warranty support as high-priority website banner claims, but verify exact wording before publishing.

## Content Rules

- Use New Zealand English.
- Keep copy practical, local, and trust-led.
- Use plain customer language for services. Include terms like "hybrid battery repair" and "hybrid battery reconditioning"; do not rely only on technical wording.
- Avoid overstating coverage beyond Glen Eden, Henderson, Kelston, New Lynn, Avondale, Titirangi, and nearby West Auckland suburbs unless the client confirms broader service demand.
- Pricing should use "starting from" language unless the client supplies fixed prices.
- Do not publish exact pricing, warranties, insurance wording, or certification language until confirmed by the client.
- Use real workshop, team, owner, and before/after photos when supplied. Avoid generic workshop imagery where possible.
