# Scrap Mart Client Workspace Instructions

This workspace is for the Scrap Mart website rebuild and SEO project.

Scrap Mart is a New Zealand scrap metal buyer and recycler. The existing website at `scrapmart.co.nz` will be replaced by a full redesign on the same domain.

## Client Context

- Public brand: Scrap Mart.
- Fireflies meeting sometimes labels the client as Battery Exchange. Use Scrap Mart for public-facing website copy unless the client confirms a legal/company naming requirement.
- Existing Auckland yard: 11 Edinburgh Street, Onehunga, Auckland.
- Christchurch branch: 48 Curries Road, Hillsborough, Christchurch 8022.
- Treat Christchurch as a confirmed operating branch for the live website plan. The new site should not say "opening soon" or treat Christchurch as draft, because the branch is expected to be running by the time the site goes live.
- Founder/owner context: Ash Sampath has worked in the recycling industry since 2011 and Scrap Mart began in 2019.
- Main commercial focus: trade and commercial scrap metal customers. Household scrap is accepted but should not lead the website.

## Website And SEO Priorities

- Lead with commercial, trade, copper, batteries, scrap prices, and local yard convenience.
- The homepage should position Scrap Mart as a scrap metal buyer for Auckland and Christchurch, with Onehunga and Hillsborough as the two main branches.
- Give Auckland and Christchurch equal strategic weight in navigation, homepage sections, price pages, local SEO, and conversion paths.
- Build for Google search, local search, AI-search visibility, and conversion.
- Pricing must be easy for non-technical staff to update weekly.
- Metal prices in this workspace are draft placeholders based on competitor research. They must be replaced or approved by the client before go-live.
- Service pages should focus on enquiries, regular commercial supply, drop-off, pickup, commercial recycling, factory scrap, industrial scrap, construction/demolition scrap, and recurring collection contracts.

## Content Rules

- Use British/New Zealand English.
- Keep the copy practical, clear, and trust-led.
- Do not overclaim national coverage. Scrap Mart is Auckland and Christchurch focused.
- Remove bin services from the website structure. Dharmesh confirmed on 24 June 2026 that the Bin Services & Sizes section should be removed.
- Do not lead with household scrap, TVs, or e-waste. The meeting made clear that household scrap is not the main business and TVs should not be highlighted.
- Do not claim solar panel recycling. The client said they do not offer this.
- Do not target car bodies, car engines, or machinery.
- Scrap Mart buys scrap batteries at both Auckland and Christchurch. Used batteries are sold only from Auckland.
- Use the wording "Free scrap metal drop-off for household appliances" where household-appliance drop-off is relevant.
- Mention same-day payment, certified weighing, compliance, environmental responsibility, and export capability where supported by context.
- AMR/NZAMR logo has been supplied by the client. Treat other licence/certification logos as pending permission unless written approval is available.
