# Jalaram Auto Client Brief

Last updated: 10 August 2026.

## Business Snapshot

Jalaram Auto is a Glen Eden automotive repair workshop based at 287C West Coast Road, Glen Eden, Auckland 0602. Public sources list Jalaram Auto as an MTA repairer, and Company Hub lists Jalaram Auto Limited as a registered New Zealand company with Chirag Rakeshbhai Thakkar as director.

The website project should position Jalaram Auto as a trusted West Auckland mechanic with a sharper SEO structure than the current service list. The highest-opportunity angle from the meeting is hybrid battery repair and reconditioning, supported by WOF, car servicing, mechanical repairs, tyres, auto electrical, and air conditioning.

## Commercial Goal

The website must generate more calls, quote requests, and bookings from local search and Google Maps, especially:

- hybrid battery repair and reconditioning enquiries
- WOF inspections and related repair work
- car servicing for petrol and hybrid vehicles
- mechanical repairs such as brakes, suspension, clutch, cam belt, transmission, and engine-related issues
- tyre replacement and puncture repair
- auto electrical and air conditioning enquiries
- WINZ quote/support enquiries, if confirmed
- standard battery and hybrid battery enquiries

## Target Customers

Primary customers:

- Glen Eden and West Auckland vehicle owners
- hybrid car owners
- customers needing WOF and WOF repair work
- family car owners comparing local mechanics
- cost-conscious customers looking for transparent starting prices
- customers with mechanical warranty or insurance-related service requirements

Secondary customers:

- nearby drivers needing puncture repair or tyre replacement
- customers looking for basic car servicing
- customers referred by word of mouth who need website reassurance before booking

## Positioning

Recommended public positioning:

> Jalaram Auto is a trusted Glen Eden mechanic for WOF checks, car servicing, hybrid battery repair, mechanical repairs, tyres, auto electrical, and air conditioning, with MTA-backed trust and honest, photo-supported repair advice.

Secondary positioning:

- Local workshop at 287C West Coast Road, Glen Eden.
- Strong focus on hybrid battery repair, rebuilding, and reconditioning.
- Genuine Google reviews should be a key proof point once current count/rating is confirmed.
- Before/after photos and old/new part photos should support trust and transparency.
- Owner-led story should help make the brand feel local, personal, and credible.

4. Mechanical Repair: Brake and Rotor Skimming, Suspension Works, Cam Belt Replacement, Clutch Works, Engine Rebuild.

9. About, Contact, and General FAQ.

Footer or contextual support items:

- WINZ quote support.
- Battery support, including auxiliary battery testing and replacement.
- Gallery and reviews once approved assets and review quotes are available.

Naming note:

- Use "Auto Electrical Repairs" as the public menu label. Use "auto electrician repair" as an SEO phrase in copy only where it reads naturally.

Lower priority or excluded:

- Wheel alignment: do not list as offered unless the client reconfirms it.
- Panel beating/minor denting: meeting context suggests this should not lead the site and may be removed.
- Used car sales: not enough context to include.

## What Matters To Customers

Common customer questions:

- Can you do my WOF?
- How much does a basic service start from?
- Can you fix hybrid battery problems?
- Can you recondition or rebuild a hybrid battery instead of replacing it?
- Are you MTA approved?
- Can you help with a mechanical warranty?
- Can you fix the repair needed after a failed WOF?
- Can this tyre puncture be repaired?
- Will you show me what was wrong before replacing parts?

## Constraints

- Do not overclaim that Jalaram Auto is the only hybrid battery repairer in the area unless independently verified and approved.
- Do not advertise wheel alignment.
- Do not lead with panel beating or denting.
- Do not publish fixed service prices until the client supplies the final price list.
- Keep exact insurance/warranty claims conservative until approved.
- Use real client photos over generic imagery.
