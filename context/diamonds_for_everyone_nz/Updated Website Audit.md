## SEO findings

### 11. Homepage lacks a useful H1

- The parsed homepage appears to contain an empty heading around the header or hero.
- H1 is not in Sequence
- Hero and navigation content use repeated lower-level engagement-ring headings.
- Add one descriptive homepage H1 based on the approved keyword map.
- Possible directions include “Lab-Grown and Natural Diamond Jewellery in New Zealand” or “Lab-Grown Diamond Engagement Rings NZ”.

### 12. Duplicate and inappropriate heading structures exist

- “Engagement Rings” appears repeatedly around header and hero structures.
- Some instances may be responsive duplicates or hidden navigation elements and require DOM inspection.
- Do not use heading elements only for styling navigation labels.
- Test rendered-but-hidden responsive structures.

### 13. Collection pages need stronger SEO introductions

- Collections such as Hidden Halo and Bezel move directly into filters and product grids.
- Add concise, useful introductions explaining the style, diamond options, shapes, metals, customisation, New Zealand delivery and consultations.
- Add supporting copy below the product grid where it helps answer buying questions.
- Avoid placing long filler content above the catalogue.

### 14. Create a collection keyword and cannibalisation map

Potentially overlapping pages include:

- Engagement Rings
- Solitaire
- Hidden Halo
- Halo
- Pave
- Three Stone
- 1ct Diamond Rings
- Diamond-shape pages
- Ring Builder
- Lab-grown diamond pages

The map should record URL, primary keyword, secondary terms, intent, parent topic, internal-link targets and competing URLs.

### 16. Product naming and metadata need a consistent system

- Creative product names such as Emerald Elegance and Royal Spark are commercially acceptable.
- Ensure descriptive search terms remain in the H1, title, metadata and visible product information.
- Do not rename every product solely for SEO; combine brand naming with clear product descriptors.

### 17. Product pages need stronger structured information

Each product page should clearly present:

- Diamond type and certification
- Centre and side-stone specifications
- Metal and purity
- Approximate production time
- Shipping, returns, warranty and resizing
- Made-to-order status
- Setting-only or complete-ring status
- What the price includes
- Care information

### 37. Ring Size Guide is a strong model to expand

The page has a New Zealand title, sizing guidance, measurement instructions, chart and international conversion information.

Use this model for:

- Engagement-ring buying guide NZ
- Lab-grown diamond price guide NZ
- Diamond shapes, clarity, colour and 4Cs guides
- Engagement-ring budget guidance
- 1ct versus 2ct comparisons
- Platinum versus white gold
- 9K versus 14K versus 18K gold
- Lab-grown versus natural diamonds NZ

### 38. Natural versus Lab-Grown page can become a stronger organic asset

Add certification, cost, resale considerations, carefully sourced environmental information, appearance, durability, New Zealand consumer context, a comparison table, FAQs and links to relevant natural and lab-grown products.

Target a clear New Zealand comparison intent without unsupported sustainability claims.

### 39. Jewellery Care page needs editorial cleanup

- Standardise headings, spelling and formatting.
- Expand diamond and engagement-ring care, white-gold maintenance, platinum care, cleaning frequency and professional inspections.
- Link recommendations to warranty terms.

### 40. Complete metadata audit as a crawl export

Record each indexable URL's:

- Status code
- Title and meta description
- H1
- Robots directives
- Canonical
- Open Graph title, description and image
- Twitter metadata
- Schema
- Word count
- Internal links

Run a full Screaming Frog or Sitebulb crawl. Do not infer full-site correctness from a browser sample.

## Content and copy findings

### 1. Australian and Brisbane content remains throughout the New Zealand website

- The homepage promotes in-person consultations at a Brisbane studio while the footer lists Level 8, 139 Quay Street, Auckland 1010.
- The About page describes a founder starting from a Brisbane suite, expansion for Australian customers and Brisbane studio appointments.
- The FAQ says the business is based in Brisbane, Australia while serving New Zealand customers.
- Confirm whether the Auckland address is a showroom, office, appointment-only location or administrative address.
- Rewrite the New Zealand About page, clarify the AU and NZ relationship, and use New Zealand-specific operating information.

Owner: Client and content team  
Priority: High

### 2. Homepage 30 Day Return claim conflicts with the policy

- The homepage advertises “30 Day Return”.
- The detailed policy excludes change-of-mind returns and mainly covers manufacturing defects or issues reported within 30 days.
- Replace the badge with accurate wording such as “Returns for Manufacturing Faults” or another client-approved statement.
- Align the claim across the homepage, product pages, FAQs and policies.

Priority: High

### 3. Contact page contains internal or draft website copy

The production page includes headings and copy such as:

- “Trust & Engagement Elements”
- “Built to establish credibility and reduce hesitation”
- “Industry Trust Badges”
- References to certifications reducing hesitation on high-value purchases
- Copy explaining that verified testimonials humanise the brand

Replace the entire block with customer-facing content such as independently certified diamonds, personal guidance, insured delivery, warranty and made-to-order service.

Priority: High

### 8. Privacy Policy uses incorrect New Zealand terminology

- The policy refers to “New Zealand Privacy Principles (APPs)”, which combines New Zealand wording with an Australian acronym.
- New Zealand privacy obligations operate under the Privacy Act 2020 and Information Privacy Principles.
- Review the policy against current requirements, including IPP 3A effective from 1 May 2026.
- Confirm disclosures for Shopify, Google, Meta, analytics, remarketing, email marketing, overseas processors, cookies, retention, access and correction rights, and privacy contact details.
- Obtain appropriate professional review.

Priority: High and compliance

### 10. Permanent 30 percent discount messaging requires verification

- Many products appear with exactly 30% off across bestseller and catalogue sections.
- Confirm whether the products were genuinely offered or sold at the crossed-out price, when that price was last charged and how long the promotion runs.
- Confirm whether Shopify compare-at pricing remains permanently enabled.
- If the promotional price has become the normal selling price, use straightforward pricing instead of a continuing discount claim.
- Review against New Zealand Commerce Commission pricing guidance.

Priority: High verification

### 15. Homepage supporting copy is too generic

- Phrases such as “Be sure to be ready for everything!” add little customer or search value.
- Replace generic taglines with concise buying guidance covering styles, settings, metals and natural or lab-grown diamond options.

### 18. Setting-only pricing needs stronger prominence

- Some products show setting-only prices while others show complete-ring prices.
- Use consistent labels such as “Setting only - centre diamond sold separately” and “Complete ring - diamond and setting included”.
- Apply the distinction across cards, collections, products, the ring builder and cart.

### 19. Homepage offer positioning is fragmented

- Visitors encounter engagement rings, custom rings, lab-grown collections, ring builder, natural diamonds, lab diamonds, gemstones and general jewellery quickly.
- Establish a primary journey, for example Design Your Engagement Ring.
- Use Shop Engagement Rings and Book a Consultation as secondary paths.
- Allow visitors to start with a setting, lab-grown diamond or natural diamond.

### 20. Call-to-action language needs standardisation

Current labels include Book Virtual Appointment, Schedule Your Appointment, Start Your Custom Design, View Diamond Options, Chat Now, Call Now, Email Us and Book a Consultation.

Create an approved CTA system, for example:

- Shopping: Shop Engagement Rings, Design Your Ring
- Consultation: Book a Consultation
- Custom work: Start Your Custom Design
- Support: Call Us, Chat on WhatsApp

### 21. Contact form CTA should be more natural

- Replace “Send Question” with “Send Enquiry” or another approved premium-brand CTA.

### 27. New Zealand English spelling is inconsistent

- Standardise jewellery, colour, discolour, personalised and authorised where appropriate.
- Replace American spellings such as jewelry and discolor.
- Run a full New Zealand English editorial review.

### 28. Capitalisation is inconsistent

Examples include HELP, ON Call, CUSTOM ORDER REQUEST, Natural Vs lab-Diamonds, Contact us and Women Wedding Band.

- Establish component-level casing rules.
- Use a consistent form such as “We're Here to Help”.

### 29. About page contains an inappropriate production note

- Remove the sentence stating that the page was inspired by appointment and consultation experiences commonly offered by premium New Zealand diamond retailers.
- This reads as an internal content-generation note rather than customer-facing copy.

Priority: High

### 30. Exceptional brilliance guaranteed requires substantiation

- Define what the guarantee legally and operationally means.
- Document eligibility, remedy and limitations, or remove “guaranteed”.
- Apply the same substantiation standard to “certified”, “best” and similar claims.

### 31. Review authenticity and source need to be clearer

- Homepage testimonials are labelled “Verified review” without an immediately visible source.
- If verified through Google, Trustpilot, Judge.me, Shopify or a purchase system, show the source or mechanism.
- Do not use the verified label when verification cannot be demonstrated.

### 32. Certification badges need explanatory context

- IGI, GIA and HRD badges may imply that the organisations certify the retailer.
- Where accurate, use wording such as “Diamonds graded by recognised independent laboratories including IGI, GIA and HRD”.
- Confirm every badge and claim with the client.

### 33. Business identity information needs clarification

The footer already provides DFE NZ PTY LIMITED, NZBN, Auckland address, email and phone. Add or clarify:

- New Zealand business status where applicable
- Appointment availability and contact hours
- Whether Auckland is a showroom, office or appointment-only location
- Relationship between the Australian and New Zealand businesses

### 45. Homepage hierarchy needs simplification

Current sections give similar weight to styles, bestsellers, shapes, trending products, collections, promise, Why Choose, testimonials and support.

Recommended order:

1. Hero
2. Engagement Ring Builder
3. Shop Engagement Rings
4. Shop by Shape
5. Best Sellers
6. Why Diamonds For Everyone
7. Custom Design Process
8. Reviews
9. Education
10. Consultation CTA

### 46. Our Promise needs accurate policy wording

Current statements include Certified Diamonds, Transparent Pricing, Custom Jewellery, Free Engraving, Free Insured Shipping and 30 Day Return.

Subject to factual confirmation, clearer alternatives include:

- Independently Certified Diamonds
- Transparent Pricing
- Made-to-Order Jewellery
- Complimentary Engraving
- Insured New Zealand Shipping
- Lifetime Manufacturing Warranty

Remove or correct the return statement.

## Technical SEO findings

### 22. Confirm that the Contact form includes a message field

- Parsed form content shows Name, Email and Phone followed by Send Question.
- If no enquiry field exists, add “What can we help you with?” and Message.
- Fire conversion tracking only after a successful submission.

### 23. Add a dedicated Thank You page and successful-submission tracking

- Create a suitable thank-you destination, such as `/pages/thank-you`.
- Track successful contact submissions separately from button clicks.
- Use a lead event for enquiries and a separate consultation-booking event.

### 34. Sold-out behaviour requires manual testing

- A sampled product exposes both sold-out and add-to-cart content in parsed HTML.
- This may be normal hidden Shopify UI, so do not label it broken without testing.
- Test sold-out default variants, available variants, variant switching, quantity, cart, diamond selection, settings, engraving and upgrades.

### 35. Custom-option pricing requires cart and order verification

- Examples include Hidden Halo +$300 and Pave Band +$540.
- Confirm that the displayed total updates, the correct price reaches the cart, options survive refresh, confirmations record selections, and analytics captures the final item price.

### 36. Ring Builder needs dedicated funnel tracking

Track the journey with separate events for:

- Ring builder start
- Setting selected
- Diamond selected
- Ring completed
- Add to cart
- Purchase

Use the data to identify abandonment by stage.

### 41. Validate Product schema across every template

Check Product, Offer, Brand, SKU, availability, price, NZD currency, URL and images. Use AggregateRating and Review only when the visible reviews and eligibility support them. Ensure schema availability matches the current variant.

### 42. Add or verify Breadcrumb schema

Use a hierarchy such as Home, Engagement Rings, Solitaire, Product. Align schema with visible breadcrumbs and the intended collection structure.

### 43. Verify Organisation or OnlineStore structured data

Include consistent business name, URL, logo, New Zealand phone, Auckland address, business identity and social profiles. Do not combine Brisbane and Auckland entities inside New Zealand schema.

### 44. Control faceted-navigation indexation

The site filters by metal, shape, price, carat, ring style, gemstone and colour. Audit:

- Canonicals
- Robots rules
- Crawl paths
- Filter links
- Sitemap inclusion
- Parameter URLs indexed by Google

Only intentionally optimised filter combinations should become indexable landing pages.

## Phased implementation plan

### Phase 1: Fix immediately

- Remove New Zealand, Australia and Brisbane contradictions.
- Correct the homepage return claim.
- Rewrite Contact page draft content.
- Remove the About page production note.
- Correct Privacy Policy terminology.
- Verify continuing 30% discount claims.
- Confirm the Auckland location and appointment status.

### Phase 2: SEO and content

- Add an approved homepage H1.
- Repair heading hierarchy.
- Optimise collection titles, descriptions and H1s.
- Add useful collection guidance.
- Build keyword and cannibalisation maps.
- Improve internal linking.
- Expand product specifications.
- Optimise educational assets.
- Standardise New Zealand English, capitalisation and grammar.

### Phase 3: Conversion

- Standardise CTA language.
- Improve the Contact form and add a Thank You page.
- Configure successful-submission and consultation events.
- Track Ring Builder stages.
- Test custom product options, sold-out variants and checkout.
- Verify reviews, certifications and trust claims.

### Phase 4: Technical verification

- Complete the full crawl.
- Audit titles, descriptions, canonicals, Open Graph metadata and schema.
- Verify sitemaps and robots directives.
- Audit filters and parameter indexation.
- Review 404s and redirects.
- Audit image weight and modern formats.
- Test Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, keyboard access and accessibility.
- Complete checkout-funnel QA.

## Bottom line

The website has a solid ecommerce base and broad catalogue, but additional SEO-page production should not be the first priority.

The correct sequence is:

1. New Zealand and Australian factual inconsistencies
2. Policy contradictions
3. Unfinished internal copy
4. Heading and template structure
5. Collection SEO and internal linking
6. Content expansion
7. Technical verification

The AU and NZ crossover appears across the homepage, About, FAQ, Contact and internal or external content. It is a sitewide credibility and localisation problem rather than one isolated typo.
