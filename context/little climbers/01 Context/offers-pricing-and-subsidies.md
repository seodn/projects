# Offers, Pricing And Subsidies

## Pricing Strategy

The client does not want exact fee tables published on the website at this stage.

Reason:

- fees vary by centre
- fees vary by age group
- fees vary by hours
- promotions vary by occupancy
- public fee numbers may create objections if a family's actual quote differs

Recommended website approach:

- explain that fees depend on centre, age, hours, and enrolment pattern
- encourage parents to book a visit or request a quote
- publish a guide about general childcare costs in Auckland
- explain 20 Hours ECE, WINZ subsidy, and FamilyBoost
- mention current centre promotions only if approved and kept updated

## 20 Hours ECE

Official source:

- Ministry of Education: https://www.education.govt.nz/early-childhood/funding-and-data/20-hours-ece-for-ece-services

Key safe facts from official source:

- Children aged 3, 4 or 5 are eligible for 20 Hours ECE.
- It covers up to 20 hours of early childhood education each week.
- It can only be used for up to 6 hours a day.
- Families need to arrange how the hours are used through the early learning service.

Little Climbers internal note:

- Transcript says Little Climbers offers 20 Hours ECE.
- Transcript says they run sessions for this and that it is not simply 10 hours across two days.
- Use simple public wording and encourage parents to ask during enquiry.

## WINZ Childcare Subsidy

Official source:

- Work and Income: https://www.workandincome.govt.nz/products/a-z-benefits/childcare-subsidy.html

Key safe facts from official source:

- The childcare subsidy can support eligible families with early childhood education costs.
- Children generally need to attend an approved service for 3 or more hours per week.
- Approved services include licensed childcare centres and approved home-based care.
- The subsidy depends on family size, income, hours, and fees.
- It is normally paid directly to the childcare provider.

Little Climbers internal note:

- Transcript says Little Climbers helps parents with WINZ forms and endorsing/submitting provider information.
- Public wording should be supportive but not guarantee eligibility.

## FamilyBoost

Official source:

- Inland Revenue: https://www.ird.govt.nz/familyboost

Key safe facts from official source:

- FamilyBoost is a childcare payment that helps eligible caregivers with ECE costs.
- Eligibility depends on children cared for, household income, and childcare costs.
- Claims are made quarterly through IRD/myIR.
- ECE providers can provide invoices or quarterly statements to support claims.

Little Climbers internal note:

- Transcript says Little Climbers should mention FamilyBoost and support families with it.
- Public wording should say families may be able to claim FamilyBoost and should check IRD eligibility.
