# Content Rules And Brand Voice

## Voice Direction

The website should feel:

- warm
- practical
- parent-focused
- trustworthy
- community-aware
- clear
- calm
- specific

Avoid:

- fluffy childcare poetry
- repeated "little explorers" style copy on every page
- keyword stuffing
- overclaiming "best"
- generic AI-sounding sentences
- making every centre sound the same
- saying things the client has not confirmed

## Preferred Language

Use:

- quality childcare
- early learning
- childcare centre
- daycare, where useful for search
- preschoolers
- toddlers
- babies/infants
- school readiness
- transition to school
- warm, homely environment
- parent partnership
- individual learning plan
- practical support for families
- pickup and drop-off at selected centres
- support with 20 Hours ECE, WINZ, and FamilyBoost

Use carefully:

- affordable childcare
- competitive fees
- small centre
- multicultural team
- qualified teachers
- Te Whariki
- vegetarian meals
- free transport

Avoid or replace:

- "best childcare in Auckland" as a primary claim
- "state-of-the-art facilities" unless proven
- "flexible hours 7am-8pm" because current facts do not support it
- "all meals included" unless per centre verified
- "all teachers are qualified" unless approved and accurate
- exact teacher-to-child ratios
- exact fees
- exact discounts unless current and approved

## Main Brand Message

Recommended positioning line:

> Quality childcare across Mangere, Otara, Dannemora and Pukekohe, with small centres, caring teachers, practical family support, and learning that reflects each child and community.

Alternative homepage headline options:

- Quality Childcare In Auckland, Built Around Families
- Childcare That Feels Personal, Practical And Local
- Small-Centre Childcare For Growing, Confident Tamariki
- Early Learning With Care, Connection And Community

## Important Approval Rules

Do not publish without client approval:

- teacher photos
- teacher cultural backgrounds
- exact staff numbers
- qualified teacher percentage
- total children served since opening
- centre capacities
- exact fees
- current offers
- exact Pukekohe transport fee
- Pukekohe infant care
- school transition partners not already confirmed

## Page Writing Rules

Every location page should include:

- the exact centre name
- address
- phone/email
- verified hours
- age groups
- transport availability
- meals
- local suburbs served
- local schools/parks/library/community ties
- what makes that centre different
- parent FAQs
- CTA to book a visit

Every programme/guide page should include:

- answer-first opening
- practical explanation for parents
- Little Climbers-specific context
- centre-specific caveats
- internal links to relevant centre pages
- FAQs
- schema recommendation

## Search And AI-Search Writing Rules

For AI-search visibility, write sections that can be lifted as standalone answers.

Good example:

"Little Climbers offers pickup and drop-off at Mangere, Otara and Pukekohe. Dannemora does not currently offer transport. Transport helps families where work schedules, one-car households, or distance make daily drop-off difficult."

Weak example:

"We make every day magical with support for your journey."

## Terms To Keep Consistent

Use:

- Mangere
- Otara
- Dannemora / East Tamaki Heights
- Pukekohe
- 20 Hours ECE
- WINZ childcare subsidy
- FamilyBoost
- pickup and drop-off
- transition visits
- transition-to-school programme

Need client choice:

- East Tamaki vs Dannemora as the main label
- daycare vs childcare centre vs early learning centre
- whether to use "tamariki" broadly on public pages

## Risk Notes

Childcare content affects parental trust. It is better to be clear and accurate than to sound impressive.

If a claim is not confirmed, write it as:

- "ask our team about..."
- "available at selected centres..."
- "depending on your child's enrolled hours..."
- "subject to centre availability..."
- "we can talk you through this during your visit..."
