## Main Strategy

Little Climbers should move away from broad "best daycare in Auckland" style messaging and towards specific, trust-building content:

- quality childcare
- small centres
- community-led care
- real centre differences
- practical support for families
- play-based learning
- individual development plans
- 20 Hours ECE, WINZ and FamilyBoost support
- transport at selected centres
- transition-to-school support

## Most Important Website Fixes

1. Rebuild each location page with unique details.
2. Fix factual conflicts around hours, age groups, transport and meals.
3. Replace generic repeated copy with specific parent answers.
4. Add a proper childcare costs/subsidies guide.
5. Add centre-specific FAQs.
6. Add proof by centre: reviews, photos, school links, staff/team where approved.
7. Align Google Business Profiles with the matching location pages.
