# Design Direction

## Design Summary

The redesigned website should feel warm, trustworthy, playful, calm and practical. It should appeal to parents while making information easy to find.

The client specifically preferred:

- pastel colours
- softer visual tone
- improved fonts
- removal of bright red elements
- playful but not childish design

## Website Personality

The design should communicate:

- quality care
- warmth
- safety
- real people
- local centres
- calm confidence
- helpfulness for busy parents

Avoid:

- overly bright red sections
- busy animated sections that distract from parent decisions
- stock-like generic childcare feel
- cluttered cards
- hiding important details below generic text

## First Screen Recommendations

The first viewport should immediately show:

- Little Climbers name/brand
- "Quality childcare..." positioning
- four-centre availability
- Book a Visit CTA
- centre finder or quick links
- trust cue, such as parent reviews or "small centres across Mangere, Otara, Dannemora and Pukekohe"

Recommended first-screen copy:

H1:

- Quality Childcare In Auckland, Built Around Families

Subcopy:

- Small, caring early learning centres in Mangere, Otara, Dannemora and Pukekohe, with practical support for children and parents.

CTA:

- Book a Visit
- Find Your Nearest Centre

## Information Architecture For Parents

Parents should be able to answer these quickly:

- Where is the centre?
- What age can my child attend?
- What are the hours?
- Do you offer transport?
- Do you provide meals?
- What does it cost?
- Can I use 20 Hours ECE/WINZ/FamilyBoost?
- How do you help children settle?
- How do I book a visit?

Design should support scanning with:

- centre comparison tables
- icon-supported feature rows
- FAQ accordions
- clear location cards
- sticky mobile CTA
- simple forms

## Location Page UX

Each location page should have:

- centre name
- address
- hours
- call button
- book visit button
- map
- age groups
- transport status
- meals status
- local suburbs served
- unique centre story
- photos
- centre-specific FAQs
- reviews for that centre

Use clear labels, not vague icons alone.

## Colour Direction

Use a soft multi-colour palette rather than one dominant colour.

Suggested palette direction:

- soft sky blue
- warm coral or muted peach
- fresh mint
- gentle yellow
- soft lavender as an accent only
- neutral off-white backgrounds
- dark readable text

Avoid:

- heavy red
- harsh primary colours
- dark corporate palette
- all-purple gradient look
- overly beige/sand palette

## Typography Direction

Use friendly, highly readable fonts.

Recommendations:

- clear sans-serif for body copy
- warm rounded display font only for headings if legibility remains strong
- avoid overly decorative fonts
- avoid very large text inside compact cards

## Content Presentation

Use practical sections:

- "Find Your Nearest Centre"
- "What Each Centre Offers"
- "How Enrolment Works"
- "Help With Fees And Subsidies"
- "How We Help Children Settle"
- "Frequently Asked Questions"

Avoid visible explanatory text about the design or features. Focus on parent actions and information.

## Conversion Improvements

Add:

- sticky call/book buttons on mobile
- one primary CTA per page
- location-specific form routing
- click-to-call tracking
- parent reassurance near forms
- short "what happens next" below forms

Form note:

- If a parent lands on a centre page, preselect that centre in the form.

## Visual Asset Needs

Request:

- exterior photo of each centre
- room photos by centre
- outdoor/playground photos
- meal photos
- van/transport photo where applicable
- staff/team photo if approved
- cultural celebration photos
- transition-to-school activity photos

Photos should look real and local. Avoid dark, overly cropped or generic stock-style images.
