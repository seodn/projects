## Warning Signs
### When should you book brake repairs?

Book a brake inspection if you notice:

- Squealing or grinding noise.
- Steering wheel vibration while braking.
- Brake pedal feels soft or low.
- Car pulls to one side.
- Longer stopping distance.
- WOF brake issue.
- Visible rotor wear or scoring.

Brakes are not a wait-and-see repair. A quick check can prevent bigger costs.

## Rotor Skimming
### What is brake rotor skimming?

Rotor skimming smooths the brake rotor surface when it is suitable to machine safely.

It can help reduce vibration, noise and uneven braking when the rotor still has enough safe thickness.
