## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Push your high-margin commercial repair pages from Page 3 to Page 1.
- **Hidden Demand:** Motorhome and Campervan Repairs.
- **Action:** Competitors dominate by mentioning specific brands and common issues (leaks, electrical, structural). Update the `/motorhome-repairs-in-christchurch/` page to explicitly mention: "We repair Swift, Jayco, and European motorhomes, specializing in water leak repairs and structural rebuilding."

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Caravan Repairs Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Caravan Repairs Christchurch | Structural & Water Leak Specialists`
- **Meta Description:** `Expert caravan repairs in Christchurch. From water damage restoration to WOF compliance and structural rebuilds, trust JL CoachBuilders to get you back on the road.`

**2. Motorhome Repairs Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Motorhome Repairs Christchurch | Certified RV Specialists`
- **Meta Description:** `Professional motorhome and RV repairs in Christchurch. We fix all major brands, handling insurance work, water leaks, and interior refits.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** In the RV/Caravan repair space, Google trusts Insurance Approvals and Compliance (WOF/COF).
- **Action:** Add a massive "Insurance Approved Repairer" banner across the top of all service pages, featuring logos of major NZ insurers (e.g., Covi, Star Insurance).

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Content Gap Attack):**
To capture the massive winter/summer prep traffic in NZ:
1. *How to Spot and Fix Caravan Water Leaks Before Winter (NZ Guide)*
2. *Caravan Solar Installation: What You Need to Know for Freedom Camping*
3. *Does Insurance Cover Caravan Delamination? (What Owners Must Know)*
4. *Top 5 Checks to Pass Your Caravan WOF on the First Try*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** `/blogs/wof-and-cof-for-caravans...` (35,000 Impressions).
- **Recipient Pages:** `/caravan-repairs-in-christchurch/` and `/motorhome-services-in-christchurch/`.
- **Exact Implementation:** **CRITICAL.** Edit the WOF blog post immediately. Add a section at the bottom: "Failed Your WOF? We Can Help." Include exact match text links: `Caravan Repairs in Christchurch` and `Motorhome Servicing`. This will pump massive ranking authority directly to those stalled pages.
