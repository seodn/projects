# BedStudio Homepage Content Brief

## Page Goal

Make BedStudio feel like a credible premium Newmarket bed and mattress showroom from the first visit.

The page should support:

- walk-in trust
- local Newmarket/Auckland visibility
- launch campaign traffic
- product discovery
- phone and enquiry actions

## Proposed H1

Premium Beds And Mattresses In Newmarket

Alternative:

BedStudio Newmarket

## Hero Message

BedStudio is a Newmarket showroom for premium beds, mattresses, bases, headboards, and customisable NZ-made options. Visit the showroom for practical advice, comfort testing, and help choosing the right bed for your room, sleep style, and budget.

## Primary CTA

Visit The Newmarket Showroom

## Secondary CTA

Call BedStudio

## Core Sections

1. Hero with showroom/product image.
2. Category cards: mattresses, bed bases, headboards, storage beds, adjustable beds.
3. Browse by size.
4. Browse by feel.
5. Browse by sleeper type.
6. NZ-made and customisable options.
7. Why visit the showroom.
8. Delivery, assembly, warranty, and payment options.
9. Launch offer block, once approved.
10. FAQs.
11. Location/contact block.

## Proof Needed

- showroom photos
- exterior signage photos
- team/staff photo
- product display photos
- approved reviews/testimonials
- confirmed hours, map link, and parking/access notes
- approved warranty/payment/offer details

## Tone

Premium, practical, clear, helpful.

Avoid:

- cheap/budget wording
- exaggerated luxury claims
- unsupported health claims
- copied Budget Beds copy
