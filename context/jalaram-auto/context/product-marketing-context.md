## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Jalaram Auto is a Glen Eden automotive repair workshop for WOF checks, car servicing, hybrid battery repair, mechanical repairs, electrical work, air conditioning, and tyres.

**What it does:** Jalaram Auto helps West Auckland drivers keep their cars roadworthy, reliable, and affordable to maintain. The workshop handles general car servicing and repairs, with a strong differentiator around hybrid battery repair, rebuilding, and reconditioning. The customer experience should be positioned around honest diagnosis, MTA-backed trust, genuine reviews, clear explanations, and before/after proof of work.

**Product category:** Local mechanic, WOF inspection, car service, hybrid mechanic, hybrid battery repair, tyre repair, auto electrical.

**Product type:** Local automotive service business.

**Business model:** Workshop service revenue from inspections, servicing, repairs, parts, tyres, standard/auxiliary batteries, hybrid battery work, and WINZ-supported quote pathways. Client-supplied pricing supports "starts from" public pricing, with GST wording confirmed for some items and still to confirm for others.

## Target Audience

**Target customers:** Private vehicle owners and hybrid vehicle owners in Glen Eden and nearby West Auckland suburbs.

**Primary areas:** Glen Eden, Henderson, Kelston, New Lynn, Avondale, Titirangi, Glendene, Green Bay, Sunnyvale, Oratia, and wider West Auckland where relevant.

**Decision-makers:** Vehicle owners, family car owners, hybrid car owners, people needing WOF or urgent repairs, and cost-conscious customers comparing local mechanics.

**Primary use case:** A driver needs a trustworthy nearby mechanic who can inspect, explain, quote, repair, and get the car back on the road without unnecessary work.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Get a WOF inspection and fix any issues needed to pass.
- Service a petrol or hybrid car at a clear starting price.
- Diagnose and repair hybrid battery problems without defaulting to an expensive full replacement.
- Fix mechanical problems such as suspension, clutch, transmission, cam belt, brakes, and engine-related repairs.
- Repair punctures or replace tyres when needed.
- Find an MTA-approved workshop that can support mechanical warranty or insurance-related servicing requirements.

**Use cases:**

- "My hybrid warning light is on and I need someone local who understands hybrid batteries."
- "I need a WOF in Glen Eden or West Auckland."
- "I need a basic service and want to know the starting price before I book."
- "My tyre has a puncture and I need to know whether it can be repaired."
- "My car failed WOF and I need the repair explained clearly."

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Local drivers need trustworthy vehicle repairs but often struggle to judge who is honest, who can handle hybrid work, and whether the final bill will be fair.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Some local mechanic sites look generic and do not clearly explain services, pricing, or proof.
- Hybrid owners may find few nearby workshops that talk directly about hybrid battery repair and reconditioning.
- Directory-style competitors can rank but may not show the individual workshop's story, evidence, or service depth.
- Some customers may assume an unfamiliar ethnic brand is less local or less established unless the site builds trust quickly.

**What it costs customers:** Time without a vehicle, failed WOF rechecks, unnecessary replacement parts, higher repair bills, uncertainty, and stress.

**Emotional tension:** Customers want the car fixed but worry about being overcharged, misunderstood, or told to replace parts without clear proof.

**Why that's better:** Customers can understand why Jalaram Auto is credible before they call, especially when they are comparing unfamiliar workshops in Glen Eden and West Auckland.

**Why customers choose Jalaram Auto:** Local convenience, trust, hybrid capability, MTA approval, genuine reviews, clear explanations, and fair starting-price expectations.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "I do not know this workshop." | Lead with MTA listing, real reviews, owner story, photos, and address in Glen Eden. |
| "Will this be expensive?" | Use starting prices, explain that final cost depends on make/model and parts, and invite quote requests. |
| "Can they handle hybrid battery issues?" | Build dedicated hybrid battery repair and reconditioning pages with symptoms, process, and repair options. |
| "Do they do WOF or only repairs?" | Make WOF a core service page and connect it to repair pages for failed inspections. |
| "Can they support mechanical warranty work?" | Mention MTA approval and documentation only with careful wording, and confirm exact warranty/insurance claims before publishing. |

**Anti-persona:** Customers looking only for the cheapest possible job regardless of oil, part quality, repair quality, or safety. Also poor fit: customers needing wheel alignment or major panel beating if those services remain excluded.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Poor communication from current mechanic, failed WOF, high dealer quotes, hybrid warning lights, slow service, or lack of trust.

**Pull:** Local Glen Eden convenience, MTA approval, hybrid battery expertise, clear pricing, genuine reviews, and owner-led accountability.

**Habit:** Customers usually return to an existing mechanic or choose whichever business appears first in Google Maps.

**Anxiety:** Fear that a new mechanic will overcharge, misdiagnose, use poor parts, or be unable to handle hybrid work.
