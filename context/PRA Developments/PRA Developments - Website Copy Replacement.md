## Reusable Copy Blocks

### PRA Standard Block

Use on homepage, About, How We Build, SEO pages, and property pages.

**Headline:** The PRA Standard

**Copy:**  
The PRA Standard is our commitment to three things buyers care about most: a quality home, a carefully selected location, and clear support through the buying journey. From plans and specifications to viewing, handover, and aftercare, PRA Developments gives buyers the information they need to make a confident decision.

**Three cards:**

| Card | Copy |
|---|---|
| Home Quality | Thoughtful layouts, quality finishes, and construction guided by selected specialists for each development. |
| Location Quality | Sites are chosen for daily convenience, nearby amenities, and long-term suitability for the people most likely to live there. |
| Buyer Support | The team helps buyers understand brochures, plans, show-home details, conditions, settlement steps, and aftercare. |

### Buyer Journey Block

**Headline:** From enquiry to handover

**Copy:**  
Buying a new-build or off-plan home is easier when the next step is clear. Start by viewing the current property information, then request the brochure, review the plans and specifications, visit the show home or walkthrough where available, ask the team your questions, and work through the agreement with your solicitor and advisers.

**Steps:**  
Explore available homes. Request the brochure. Review plans and specifications. View the home or walkthrough. Confirm questions and conditions. Move through contract, settlement, and handover.

### Investor-Safe Copy Block

Use wherever investment language appears.

**Copy:**  
For investors, each PRA development should be assessed on its own details: purchase price, location, specifications, expected rental demand, body corporate or ownership structure where relevant, rates, insurance, and lending position. PRA can provide current property information and connect you with the right sales contact, but buyers should seek independent financial, legal, and tax advice before making a decision.

### Off-Plan Explanation Block

**Copy:**  
An off-plan purchase means you are buying from plans, specifications, and agreed contract documents before the home is complete. PRA helps buyers understand what is included by providing the latest brochure, plans, specification details, representative or property-specific media, and opportunities to ask questions before committing.

### Aftercare Block

Use only after PRA confirms the written terms.

**Copy:**  
After settlement, PRA provides handover information so buyers know how to look after their new home and who to contact if something needs attention. Defect support, appliance warranties, timeframes, exclusions, and reporting steps are governed by the confirmed handover documents for the specific development.

### Replace Repeated `Why Buy` Sections
