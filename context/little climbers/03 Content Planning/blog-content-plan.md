# Blog And Guide Content Plan

## Content Strategy

The content hub should answer high-value parent questions that competitors already benefit from, while using Little Climbers' real strengths:

- small centres
- transport at selected centres
- meals
- school readiness
- subsidy support
- individual development plans
- community-specific care

## First 10 Priority Guides

### 1. Childcare Costs In Auckland: Fees, 20 Hours ECE, WINZ And FamilyBoost

Goal:

- rank for childcare cost searches and educate price-sensitive parents without publishing exact fees.

Include:

- what affects fees
- why age and hours matter
- 20 Hours ECE
- WINZ subsidy
- FamilyBoost
- why quotes are centre-specific
- how to ask for a fee estimate

Internal links:

- homepage
- all centre pages
- contact/book a visit

### 2. 20 Hours ECE Explained For Auckland Parents

Goal:

- answer subsidy questions clearly and build trust.

Include:

- who it applies to
- weekly and daily limits
- how it fits with childcare hours
- why parents still need to talk to the centre

### 3. WINZ Childcare Subsidy And FamilyBoost: What Parents Should Know

Goal:

- become a helpful parent resource.

Include:

- eligibility is not guaranteed
- where to check
- what documents parents may need
- how the centre can help with provider details

### 4. What Age Should My Child Start Daycare?

Goal:

- answer a sensitive parent concern without judgement.

Angle:

- the right age depends on family needs, work, the child, and centre availability
- childcare can support social development
- transition visits help
- younger children can settle with the right support

Avoid:

- saying parents should keep children home until a specific age.

### 5. How To Choose A Childcare Centre In Auckland

Goal:

- rank for decision-stage searches and steer parents toward Little Climbers' strengths.

Include:

- safety
- teacher interaction
- routines
- meals
- curriculum
- parent communication
- transition support
- location and transport
- fees and subsidies

### 6. What To Ask During A Childcare Centre Tour

Goal:

- support centre-visit conversion.

Include:

- questions about age groups, meals, routines, settling, ratios, school readiness, communication, fees, subsidies, allergies and transport.

### 7. How To Help Your Child Settle Into Daycare

Goal:

- answer one of the biggest parent fears.

Include:

- transition visits
- calm goodbye
- do not disappear without telling the child
- consistency
- signs of settling
- every child is different

### 8. What To Do If Your Child Cries At Daycare Drop-Off

Goal:

- rank for high-emotion parenting queries.

Include:

- why crying happens
- how teachers support children
- what parents can do
- when to ask for extra support

### 9. How Childcare Helps Children Get Ready For School

Goal:

- support preschool and transition-to-school positioning.

Include:

- communication skills
- self-help skills
- social skills
- routines
- belonging
- school visits/relationships where confirmed

### 10. Daycare vs Preschool vs Kindergarten In New Zealand

Goal:

- capture comparison searches and explain terms clearly.

Include:

- plain-English definitions
- what Little Climbers offers
- age/stage caveats
- how to choose based on family need
