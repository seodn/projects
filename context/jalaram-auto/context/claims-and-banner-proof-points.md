## Banner Claims To Use Once Verified

These are the high-visibility claims that should appear across the site once confirmed:

- MTA approved repairer.
- WOF / warrant of fitness available, exact authority wording to confirm.
- Afterpay available.
- Mechanical warranty support available, exact wording to confirm.
- Workmanship guarantee or service guarantee, exact coverage and wording to confirm.
- Genuine Google reviews, current count and rating to confirm.
- Before/after photos provided for repairs where useful.
- Old parts and repair issues explained clearly to customers.
- Hybrid battery repair and reconditioning available.
- WINZ quotes/support available, exact process to confirm.

## Important Note On "FDA Approved"

The meeting context appears to reference "MTA approved", not "FDA approved". For an automotive workshop in New Zealand, MTA approval is the relevant trust claim found in public context. Do not use "FDA approved" on the website unless the client explicitly confirms a separate claim and provides proof.

## Claims To Avoid Until Verified

- "FDA approved."
- "Only hybrid battery repairer in Glen Eden."
- "Guaranteed cheapest mechanic."
- "Guaranteed insurance approval."
- "All repairs guaranteed" without exclusions.
- "Same-day repair" unless service-specific capacity is confirmed.
- Exact Google review count or rating without a current audit.
