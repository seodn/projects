# Delivery, Warranty, Payments And FAQ Notes

## Delivery

Meeting notes from 9 July 2026 mention:

- Auckland delivery is available on set days.
- New Zealand-wide delivery is possible through Mainfreight or a main freight provider.
- South Auckland delivery days were discussed as Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.
- West Auckland delivery days were discussed as Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday.
- Delivery within a 20 km radius was discussed at around `$89`.
- Delivery outside that radius was discussed around `$110-$125`, depending on distance.

Confirm all delivery areas, days, costs, and wording before publishing.

## Assembly And Setup

Meeting notes mention:

- Most bed bases come built up.
- Customers generally only need to install legs.
- Electric bed bases may need assembly support.
- Assembly/setup can be provided at extra cost.
- Assembly/setup was discussed around `$30-$40`.
- A one-hour paid assembly option may be available on request.
- Delivery teams should confirm the room location before placing goods.

Confirm final setup pricing before publishing.

## Old Mattress And Base Removal

Meeting notes mention:

Confirm final removal policy, pricing, and availability before publishing.

## Warranty

Meeting notes mention:

- NZ-made mattresses may carry up to 10 years warranty.
- Some other products may carry around 5 years warranty.
- Imported mattresses may carry around 24 months warranty.

Product-specific warranty must be confirmed before publishing. Avoid blanket warranty claims across all products.

## Returns
