## 6. Editorial rules from the meeting

1. Keep “PRA standard”; do not invent a new full form.
2. Exclude the dropped historical purchase-price/current-valuation case study.
3. Do not equate gross rental yield with property appreciation, guaranteed profit, or compounded returns.
4. Do not publish blanket deposit percentages or claim PRA provides finance. Verify local lending wording and distinguish it from contractual deposit terms.
5. Keep competitor names and specific sales-agency names out of public copy.
6. Avoid unsupported credentials, universal appliance warranty durations, community headcounts, rankings, and savings.
7. Treat “top player” as an ambition; no numerical one-, two-, or five-year targets were agreed.
8. Write proper NZ and AU content rather than substituting location words. Check claims separately by market.
9. Paiman reviews the draft before website publication. Saving the working context to GitHub does not satisfy client copy approval.
10. Begin the planned PR activity after the website content update.
