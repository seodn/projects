## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Push your high-margin local service query (`phone repair pukekohe`) into the top 3 spots.
- **Hidden Demand:** Screen replacements, battery fixes in the specific Pukekohe area.
- **Action:** The site is structurally dominated by `/collections/` (e-commerce). We need to elevate the "Repair" entity. Add a massive, dedicated section on the homepage specifically titled "Fast & Reliable Phone Repair in Pukekohe", and ensure it links to a dedicated local repair page (not just a contact form).

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Homepage**
- **Title Tag:** `Tech Surgeon | Phone Repair Pukekohe & Unlocked Mobiles`
- **Meta Description:** `Pukekohe's trusted experts for fast screen replacements, battery repairs, and tech support. We also sell premium unlocked and refurbished mobile phones. Visit us today!`

**2. Nokia Collections Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Nokia Mobile Phones NZ | Classic & Flip Phones Unlocked`
- **Meta Description:** `Looking for a reliable Nokia? Shop our range of classic, flip, and modern Nokia mobile phones in NZ. Unlocked and ready to use. Fast shipping available.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** For repair services (where people are handing over their expensive devices), Google and users want to see physical security and expertise.
- **Action:** Embed your Google Maps location directly on the Homepage. Add "90-Day Warranty on All Repairs" prominently above the fold. 

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Listicles for Content Gap Attack):**
To capture top-of-funnel traffic for your repair services and niche device sales:
1. *7 Things to Do Immediately If You Drop Your Phone in Water*
2. *Top 5 Reasons the Classic Nokia Flip Phone is Making a Massive Comeback in NZ*
3. *10 Signs Your iPhone Needs a Battery Replacement Right Now*
4. *5 Ways to Protect Your Smartphone Screen from Cracking*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** Homepage (`/`).
- **Recipient Pages:** Your dedicated Pukekohe repair service page.
- **Exact Implementation:** The homepage has 10,000+ impressions but is bleeding authority to random product collections. You must place an exact match text link `Phone Repair Pukekohe` high up on the homepage, pointing directly to your repair service information, to signal to Google that this is your core local entity.
