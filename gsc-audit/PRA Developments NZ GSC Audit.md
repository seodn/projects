## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Increase impressions + rankings for pages stuck on Page 2 and 3.
- **Hidden Demand:** Queries like `investment property for sale` and `property investment christchurch` are stuck on Page 3. Google is testing you for these, but you lack dedicated intent mapping. 
- **Action:** These 3 actions will increase traffic fastest:
  1. Build a dedicated `/investors/` hub page.
  2. Map 3 contextual internal links with the anchor "Christchurch property investment" pointing to this new hub from your homepage.
  3. Expand the H2 headings on `/townhouses-in-christchurch` to specifically mention "Turnkey Townhouses" and "New Builds".

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.
**Pages Leaking Clicks:** `/townhouses-in-christchurch` (7,510 Imp, 0.75% CTR) and `/2-bedroom-houses-in-christchurch` (4,788 Imp, 1.38% CTR).

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Townhouses in Christchurch**
- **Title Tag:** `2026 Townhouses For Sale in Christchurch | New Builds | PRA`
- **Meta Description:** `Explore our exclusive range of brand new townhouses for sale across Christchurch. From affordable first homes to luxury investments. View current pricing and plans today!`

**2. 2 Bedroom Houses in Christchurch**
- **Title Tag:** `2 Bedroom Houses For Sale Christchurch | Turnkey Homes | PRA`
- **Meta Description:** `Looking for a modern 2-bedroom house in Christchurch? Discover our premium new build homes perfect for first home buyers and savvy investors. Secure yours now.`

**3. Properties in Merivale**
- **Title Tag:** `Luxury Properties & Houses For Sale in Merivale | PRA`
- **Meta Description:** `Discover premium, high-end properties for sale in Merivale, Christchurch. Architecturally designed homes built for luxury living in the city's finest suburb.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** Google increasingly trusts this topic cluster: *Residential New Builds and 2-Bedroom Homes*. 
- **Query Drift:** Google is trying to rank you for "property developers christchurch" (Pos 9.1) which is highly lucrative, indicating strong root domain trust.

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Build these 4 Core Hub Pages next:**

## Additional Intelligence Layers

### 1. Business Value & Commercial Intent Scoring
Queries containing commercial modifiers ("for sale", "developers", "investment") have been prioritized. `property developers christchurch` and `townhouses for sale christchurch` score a **9.5/10** for Lead Potential (Bottom of Funnel).

### 2. Implementation Readiness & Execution Intelligence
Moving beyond theory to exact implementations:
- **Exact Missing Entities:** The `townhouses-in-christchurch` page is missing the entities: *Master Build Guarantee, Turnkey, Freehold Title, Body Corporate*. 
- **Exact FAQ Suggestion:** Add: *"Do your townhouses in Christchurch come with a Master Build Guarantee?"*
- **Exact Heading Recommendation:** Change `Why Buy New Townhouses in Christchurch` to `Why Buy Turnkey Townhouses in Christchurch`.

### 3. Semantic Internal Link Modelling & Authority Hub Detection
- **Authority Hub Missing:** You do not have a centralized `/properties/` hub that passes semantic relevance down to `/properties/merivale` or `/properties/riccarton`. The site relies on a flat structure, diluting authority.

### 4. Content Format Intelligence
- **Winning Competitors Use:** Pricing tables, interactive maps, and side-by-side comparisons of floor plans.
- **Your Format:** Dense text paragraphs. 
- **Recommendation:** Break up the "Where Culture Meets Community" block into bullet points highlighting *Schools, Transport, and ROI*.

### 5. User Journey Modelling
- **Current Flow:** User lands on `/townhouses-in-christchurch` → Read dense text → Must scroll to the bottom to find "Contact Us".
- **Ideal Flow:** User lands → Sees dynamic property grid (Available Now vs Sold Out) → Clicks Floor Plan → Enquires on specific property.

### 6. Index Quality Engine (Index Hygiene Analysis)
- **Soft Duplicate URLs:** The `/?utm_source=google-business` URL is receiving impressions. Ensure canonical tags point `/?utm_source=...` strictly to `/`. 
- **URL Structure:** Flat hierarchy (`/properties-in-riccarton`). Migrate to `/locations/riccarton/` or `/properties/riccarton/` to prevent crawl budget waste and cannibalization as you add more suburbs.

### 7. Site Quality Scoring
- **Crawl Quality:** 9/10 (Next.js is clean).
- **Index Hygiene:** 7/10 (UTM parameters indexing).
- **Semantic Depth:** 5/10 (Missing critical real estate entities).
- **Intent Alignment:** 4/10 (Mixing investors with first-home buyers).
- **CTR Efficiency:** 3/10 (Bleeding clicks heavily).

### 8. Content-to-Title Consistency Analysis
- Your proposed new titles promise "New Builds" and "Luxury & Affordable". Ensure the actual landing pages prominently display the words *New Builds* and clear *Pricing Brackets* so the user intent is instantly satisfied upon clicking.

### 9. Semantic Coverage Validation
- For the query `property investment christchurch`, your site must mention *Rental Yields, Tax Deductibility (New Build Exemption), Capital Growth, and Depreciation*. Currently, these entities are largely missing from the core service pages.

### 11. Competitor Sitemap & Content Gap Engine
- **Competitor:** *Wolfbrook Residential* & *South Projects*
- **Sitemap Discovery:** Competitors are not just building location pages. They are building massive **"Investor Guides"** and **"First Home Buyer Guides"**. They capture top-of-funnel traffic (e.g., "how much deposit do I need for a townhouse in NZ") and funnel it down to their property listings.
- **Output:** This competitor is also capturing traffic with related content hubs (Calculators, Area Profiles, Investment Case Studies). We should build a **"Christchurch Property Investment Guide"** to attract this same traffic.
