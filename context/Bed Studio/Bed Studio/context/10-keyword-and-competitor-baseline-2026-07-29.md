Checked: 29 July 2026

No verified Search Console export or rank-tracker report was available. Exact positions must therefore be recorded as `not confirmed`, not zero.

## Market Priority

1. Newmarket: physical showroom and local-intent searches.
2. Auckland: collection and commercial searches.
3. New Zealand: selected product, NZ-made and buying-guide opportunities where delivery and product availability support the claim.

## Competitor Snapshot

- Bedpost Newmarket: strong location/store footprint and established retail trust.
- Beds R Us and Sealy Newmarket: recognised retail/brand presence around Newmarket.
- HEKA Newmarket: premium positioning and strong showroom relevance.
- Sleep Gallery, Simply Beds and Natural Beds: Auckland/NZ mattress range and specialist messaging.
- Napp: visible adjustable-bed proposition.
- Ecosa and NZ Beds: established sleeper-led educational content.

## Bed Studio Opportunity

Bed Studio should combine local showroom credibility with:

- NZ-made and customisable choices where factually true.
- clear Size, Feel and Sleeper Type shopping paths.
- real person/showroom advice.
- product comparison content.
- delivery, warranty and finance clarity once confirmed.
- original showroom and product proof.

## Measurement Setup

- Add and verify Google Search Console.
- Track the agreed Newmarket and Auckland terms weekly.
- Record Google Business Profile calls, directions and website clicks.
- Track calls, forms, add-to-cart, checkout and purchase events where enabled.
- Report landing page, collection and product performance separately.
- Save the first verified report as the formal baseline before SEO changes are evaluated.
