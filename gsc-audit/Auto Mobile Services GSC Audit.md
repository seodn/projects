**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** In the mechanic space, Google trusts transparency (pricing, guarantees) and official accreditations (MTA approved, WINZ registered).
- **Action:** Ensure your "WINZ Registered Provider" badge is highly visible on the homepage above the fold.
