# Scrap Mart Client Brief

Last updated: 26 June 2026.

## Business Snapshot

Scrap Mart is a scrap metal buyer and recycling yard based in Onehunga, Auckland. The business started in 2019 after taking over the established Warren Metals site. The website is being rebuilt on the existing domain, `scrapmart.co.nz`, as part of a new SEO programme.

The client has confirmed the Christchurch branch at 48 Curries Road, Hillsborough, Christchurch 8022. Treat Christchurch as a full branch in the live website plan, not as a draft or "opening soon" location.

## Commercial Goal

The website must generate more commercial and trade enquiries, especially:

- regular scrap supply from trades and businesses
- commercial scrap collection enquiries
- copper, cable, battery, aluminium, and mag wheel sellers
- Christchurch branch enquiries and Google Ads traffic

Household scrap can stay on the website, but it should not lead the main navigation or homepage messaging.

## Target Customers

Primary customers:

- electricians
- plumbers
- air conditioning and heat pump installers
- mechanics and auto dismantlers
- manufacturing and fabrication businesses
- construction and demolition contractors
- factories and workshops
- businesses with regular scrap metal volumes

Secondary customers:

- household customers with acceptable metal items
- people dropping off old batteries, appliances, or small mixed metal loads

Fireflies context says the business is roughly 95 percent commercial/trade and 5 percent household. Treat this as strategy context, not a published statistic unless the client approves it.

## Positioning

Recommended public positioning:

> Scrap Mart buys scrap metal from tradies, businesses, and households in Auckland and Christchurch, with competitive weekly prices, fair certified weighing, and fast same-day payment.

Secondary positioning:

- Auckland established yard in Onehunga.
- Christchurch branch at 48 Curries Road, Hillsborough.
- Christchurch hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 4:30pm.
- Christchurch phone: 02041234569.
- Strong focus on copper, batteries, cables, aluminium, mag wheels, and commercial scrap.
- Clear weekly pricing by metal grade.
- Easy drop-off, pickup, and commercial collection options.

Confirmed or strongly supported:

- Established in 2019.
- Ash Sampath has worked in the recycling industry since 2011.

Needs confirmation before publishing strongly:

## What Matters To Customers

Common customer questions from Fireflies:

- What is the price?
- Do you accept this metal or item?
- Can you pick it up?
- Can I get paid today?
- How do you grade the material?
- What ID do I need?

Website must answer these clearly and close to the enquiry path.

## Constraints

- Do not overemphasise household scrap.
- Do not lead with TVs or e-waste. E-waste appears on the supplied price-list commodity file as POA, so keep it secondary and careful.
- Do not offer solar panel recycling.
- Remove bin services from the current website structure. Dharmesh confirmed on 24 June 2026 that the Bin Services & Sizes section should be removed.
- Do not target car bodies, car engines, or machinery.
- Scrap Mart buys scrap batteries at both Auckland and Christchurch. Used batteries are sold only from Auckland.
- Use "Free scrap metal drop-off for household appliances" where household-appliance drop-off is relevant.
- Prohibited items confirmed by client: asbestos, oil, lithium batteries, alkaline batteries, bio waste, gas bottles, and flammable liquids.
- Christchurch should receive equal strategic weight with Auckland in the new website structure.
- Do not use "opening soon" wording for Christchurch in the live website plan.
- Weekly metal prices are time-sensitive and must be editable.
