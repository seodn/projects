# Little Climbers Context Index

Last updated: 3 July 2026

## What This Pack Is For

This folder gives the Digital Neighbour team a single source of working context for Little Climbers. It combines:

- Fireflies meeting transcript insights from 13 and 16 June 2026
- the current Little Climbers website
- competitor and market observations
- official NZ childcare support references
- SEO and AI-search page planning
- content and design direction

## Read This First

1. `client-brief.md` gives the business, audience, positioning, and strategic summary.
2. `location-and-service-area.md` gives centre-by-centre facts.
3. `services-and-programmes.md` gives the care, curriculum, meals, transport, subsidies, and parent journey details.
4. `content-rules-and-brand-voice.md` explains what to say, what to avoid, and what needs approval.
5. `evidence-bank.md` stores reusable proof points and what proof is still missing.
6. `offers-pricing-and-subsidies.md` explains the pricing approach, offers, 20 Hours ECE, WINZ, and FamilyBoost.
7. `open-questions.md` is the client follow-up list.

## Status Summary

The project is ready for content planning and wireframing, but not fully ready for final publishing. The transcript gives strong detail, but several facts need client confirmation before they appear on the live website.

Highest-risk gaps:

- operating hours conflict between transcript and current website for Otara and Pukekohe
- teacher-cultural-background claims need consent
- pricing/offers should remain generic unless approved
- meal details vary by centre and need final wording
- school transition partners are incomplete for Otara and Pukekohe
