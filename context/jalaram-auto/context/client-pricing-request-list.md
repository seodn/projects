# Jalaram Auto Client Pricing Request List

Last updated: 4 August 2026.

Status: Detailed pricing checklist to send to the client. Use "starting from" pricing on the website unless the client confirms fixed public prices.

## Pricing Rules To Confirm

- Which prices can be published publicly?
- Should prices include GST or show "plus GST"?
- Should the site say "starting from" for every service?
- Are diagnostics charged separately?
- Are parts always extra unless stated?
- Are prices different for sedan/hatchback, SUV, van, European vehicles, diesel vehicles, hybrid vehicles, or commercial vehicles?
- Should the website mention that final pricing depends on make, model, parts, and inspection?

## WOF

Ask client for:

- WOF inspection price.
- WOF recheck price, if any.
- Failed WOF repair quote process.
- Whether WOF repairs can be bundled or discounted.
- Any common WOF repair starting prices.

## Car Servicing

Meeting context mentioned basic servicing around `$120 plus GST` for sedan/hatchback and `$150 plus GST` for SUV, but this must be confirmed.

Ask client for:

- Basic service starting price for sedan/hatchback.
- Basic service starting price for SUV.
- Basic service starting price for van/ute, if offered.
- Full service starting price for sedan/hatchback.
- Full service starting price for SUV.
- Hybrid car service starting price.
- Diesel service starting price, if applicable.
- What each service includes: oil, oil filter, fluid checks, safety checks, scan, tyre pressure, report, labour.
- Oil grades/brands to mention or avoid.

## Mechanical Repairs

Ask client for starting prices or quote rules for:

- Cam belt replacement.
- Brake pads.
- Brake rotors.
- Brake and rotors skimming.
- Suspension works.
- Shock absorbers.
- Clutch repair/replacement.
- Transmission work.
- Rebuild engine / engine rebuild.
- Rebuilt engine replacement.
- Diagnostic scan.
- Labour hourly rate, if public.

## Electrical Repair

Ask client for:

- Electrical diagnostic fee.
- Battery warning light diagnosis.
- Starter motor repair/replacement starting price.
- Alternator repair/replacement starting price.
- Wiring fault diagnosis starting price.
- Any electrical jobs that are outsourced and should not be advertised.

## Air Conditioning Repair

Ask client for:

- Air conditioning inspection price.
- Air conditioning regas price.
- Leak test price.
- AC repair labour or starting price.
- Whether refrigerant type affects pricing.
- Whether AC work is in-house or outsourced.

## Tyre

Ask client for:

- Puncture repair price.
- New tyre starting price.
- Tyre fitting price.
- Wheel balancing price, if offered.
- Valve replacement price, if offered.
- Tyre disposal fee, if applicable.
- Tyre brands stocked or supplied.
- Clear rule for sidewall punctures not being repairable.

## Battery

Ask client for:

- Standard car battery testing price.
- Standard car battery replacement starting price.
- Auxiliary battery pricing.
- Hybrid battery diagnostic price.
- Hybrid battery repair starting price.
- Hybrid battery reconditioning starting price.
- Hybrid battery replacement starting price.
- Warranty/guarantee terms for battery work.
- Makes/models covered, especially Toyota hybrid models if relevant.

## Hybrid

Ask client for:

- Hybrid diagnostic fee.
- Hybrid car service starting price.
- Hybrid battery repair starting price.
- Hybrid battery reconditioning starting price.
- Hybrid battery rebuild starting price.
- Hybrid battery replacement starting price.
- Whether pricing changes by vehicle model.
- What warranty or guarantee applies to hybrid battery work.
- Whether used/reconditioned hybrid battery options are available.

## WINZ

Ask client for:

- Does Jalaram Auto accept WINZ quotes/payment support?
- What information does the customer need to provide?
- Does the workshop provide a written quote for WINZ?
- Are there any minimum/maximum job values?
- Does the customer need approval before work begins?
- What wording can be used publicly?

## Payment Options

Ask client to confirm:

- Afterpay.
- EFTPOS/card.
- Online banking.
- Cash.
- Finance options.
- Deposit requirements.
- Payment due timing.
- Surcharges, if any.

## Guarantee And Warranty

Ask client to confirm:

- Workmanship guarantee wording.
- Parts warranty wording.
- Hybrid battery warranty wording.
- Tyre warranty wording.
- Air conditioning warranty wording.
- What is excluded from guarantees?
- Whether warranty length differs by service.
- Mechanical warranty/insurance provider process and wording.

## Website Pricing Display Recommendation

Use cards or tables with:

- Service name.
- Starting price.
- "From" qualifier.
- What is included.
- What affects final price.
- CTA: "Ask for a quote."

Example format after approval:
