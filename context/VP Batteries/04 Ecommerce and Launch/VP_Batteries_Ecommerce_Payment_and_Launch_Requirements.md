Ecommerce, Payment and Launch Requirements

Recommendation

Use the existing WordPress and WooCommerce foundation if a staging health check confirms it is secure and maintainable.

Do not begin with unrestricted online battery shipping. Launch a controlled hybrid model:

1. Enquiry and vehicle-confirmation for every automotive battery.
2. Online payment for approved SKUs and fulfilment methods.
3. Workshop pickup and clearly zoned local delivery first.
4. Mobile fitting booked or confirmed by the team.
5. Trade-account ordering handled separately from retail checkout.

Customer journeys

Urgent mobile

Call or short form → confirm location and vehicle → test/quote → attend if available → fit → payment → recycle old battery.

Workshop retail

Find a battery or call → vehicle confirmation → workshop test → product and warranty explanation → fit/pay → recycle.

Online eligible product

Select product → enter vehicle/application details → compatibility acknowledgement or staff approval → choose fulfilment → pay → confirmation and tax invoice.

Trade

Trade enquiry → business verification and approved terms → account pricing/order method → delivery/pickup → invoice/account payment.

Product eligibility gate

A product may be sold online only when all are complete:

- canonical SKU;
- correct title and image;
- current price and GST treatment;
- current stock or lead-time rule;
- compatibility requirements;
- warranty and use-case exclusions;
- weight and dimensions;
- allowed fulfilment methods;
- fitting availability;
- approved returns handling;
- active product status.

All other products should use Enquire now or Confirm fitment.

Payment requirements

- Select a New Zealand-supported gateway after comparing account ownership, fees, settlement, refunds and WooCommerce compatibility.
- Use hosted card entry. Do not store card numbers on the VP Batteries site.
- Enable test mode first.
- Confirm accepted cards and any bank-transfer/account options.
- Configure GST tax invoices.
- Restrict administrator access and use multi-factor authentication.
- Record who can refund, change prices and view orders.

Trade credit

If VP Batteries wants online credit applications, obtain approved legal and accounting requirements before building the form. Potential fields include:

- legal business name and NZBN;
- trading name;
- addresses and contacts;
- accounts-payable email;
- requested credit limit and terms;
- trade references;
- director/guarantor details where legally approved;
- consent to credit checks;
- signed terms.

Do not collect sensitive credit information in a normal contact form or email attachment without an approved secure process.

Fulfilment rules

Define:

- workshop pickup hours;
- Christchurch delivery zones;
- Canterbury mobile zones;
- after-hours rules;
- South Island freight-eligible products;
- dangerous-goods carrier and packaging;
- delivery price and free-delivery thresholds, if any;
- what happens if the customer selects the wrong battery;
- whether old-battery return is required or optional.

Required policy pages

- Terms and conditions.
- Privacy policy.
- Delivery and pickup.
- Returns and cancellations.
- Warranty policy.
- Trade account terms.
- Mobile service and callout terms.
- Battery safety and recycling.

The current San Francisco returns placeholder must be removed.

Staging launch checklist

1. Full backup and rollback plan.
2. WordPress, theme, plugin, PHP and licence audit.
3. Canonical product import.
4. Price, GST and stock approval.
5. Approved pages and policies.
6. Payment gateway in test mode.
7. Test successful and failed payments.
8. Test refund, cancellation and stock restoration.
9. Test pickup, delivery and mobile-service orders.
10. Test retail and trade emails.
11. Confirm invoice details.
12. Check mobile layout, performance and accessibility.
13. Validate analytics, call tracking and ecommerce events.
14. Validate redirects, canonicals, sitemap, robots and noindex rules.
15. Client sign-off and timed launch.

Phase-two opportunities

- Vehicle registration or make/model battery finder.
- Trade login and account reordering.
- Saved fleet vehicles.
- Automated low-stock notifications.
- Review request after completed orders.
- Service-zone pricing automation.
