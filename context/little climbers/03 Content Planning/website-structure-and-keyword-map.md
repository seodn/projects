## Homepage Brief

Primary goal:

- explain Little Climbers' value and send parents to the right centre.

Recommended H1:

- Quality Childcare In Auckland, Built Around Families

Core sections:

1. Hero with centre finder and Book a Visit CTA
2. Four centre cards with suburb/address/hours summary
3. Why Little Climbers: small centres, personal relationships, practical family support
4. What families need: transport, meals, care stages, transition support
5. Age groups: babies/infants, toddlers, preschoolers, with centre caveats
6. Parent journey: enquire, visit, discuss fees/support, transition visits, enrolment
7. Affordability: 20 Hours ECE, WINZ, FamilyBoost
8. Founder/about preview
9. Reviews/testimonials
10. FAQs
11. CTA: book a visit

## Location Architecture

Each location page should be unique. Suggested URL patterns:

- `/childcare-centre-mangere/`
- `/childcare-centre-otara/`
- `/childcare-centre-east-tamaki/`
- `/childcare-centre-pukekohe/`

If the final brand prefers "Dannemora", consider:

- `/childcare-centre-dannemora-east-tamaki/`

Do not create separate pages for every nearby suburb yet. Use nearby suburbs within the relevant centre pages.

## Internal Link Rules

Homepage:

- link to all four centre pages
- link to fees/subsidies guide
- link to transition page

Location pages:

- link back to homepage and Our Centres
- link to transition page
- link to fees/subsidies guide
- link to relevant age-group pages

Guide pages:

- link to relevant centre pages
- link to Book a Visit
- link to subsidies/fees page where relevant

## Pages To Avoid For Now

Avoid creating thin pages for:

- childcare Papatoetoe
- childcare Otahuhu
- childcare Manurewa
- childcare Botany
- childcare Flat Bush
- childcare Pokeno
- childcare Drury

These can be sections inside the main location pages until there is enough unique proof and content.
