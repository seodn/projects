## 5 Master Decision Systems

### A. Traffic Growth System (Stalled Queries)
**Purpose:** Increase impressions + rankings for pages stuck in the Page 2/3 blackhole.
- **Hidden Demand:** The Homepage (`/`) and Wedding (`/wedding/`) pages.
- **Action (Homepage):** Change the current H1 from "Flowery Expressions" to "**Best Florist in Christchurch - Fresh Flowers Delivered Daily**". 

### B. CTR / SERP Win System (Leaking Clicks)
**Purpose:** Increase clicks without rankings changing by optimizing metadata.

**Exact Implementation (Copy & Paste these into your CMS):**

**1. Homepage**
- **Title Tag:** `Christchurch City Flowers | Premium Florist & Same Day Delivery`
- **Meta Description:** `Looking for the best florist in Christchurch? Shop stunning flower bouquets for graduation, weddings, and funerals. Order now for fast, same-day delivery!`

**2. Wedding Flowers Page**
- **Title Tag:** `Wedding Flowers Christchurch | Bridal Bouquets & Floral Styling`
- **Meta Description:** `Make your big day unforgettable with premium wedding flowers in Christchurch. From bespoke bridal bouquets to venue styling, book your consultation today.`

### C. Google Trust System
**Purpose:** Understand what Google trusts.
- **Observation:** In the florist industry, Google trusts real images (not stock), local delivery grids, and Google Review embeddings.
- **Action:** Embed a live feed of your Google Business Reviews directly onto the homepage and the wedding page. Add a "Same Day Delivery Cut-off Time: 2 PM" badge sitewide to build transactional trust.

### D. Content Investment System
**Purpose:** Tell the business WHAT content to create next based on commercial opportunity.

**Recommended Blog / Article Titles (Content Gap Attack):**
To capture top-of-funnel floral traffic and build trust with Christchurch locals, publish these highly-searched informational articles:
1. *The Ultimate Guide to Choosing Graduation Flowers & Corsages in Christchurch*
2. *Top 5 Wedding Flower Trends in NZ for the Upcoming Season*
3. *How to Care for Your Fresh Flower Bouquet to Make it Last Longer*
4. *A Guide to Funeral Flower Etiquette: What to Send and When*

### E. Authority Flow System & Semantic Link Modelling
**Purpose:** Redistribute ranking power strategically.
- **Donor Page:** `/graduation/corsage/` and `/about-us/`.
- **Recipient Pages:** `/wedding/` and `/shop/`.
- **Exact Implementation:** Funnel authority from your successful graduation/about pages by adding internal links back to the main `/shop/` directory.
