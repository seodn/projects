# FAQ And AI Answer Bank

## Which Little Climbers centres offer pickup and drop-off?

Little Climbers offers pickup and drop-off at Mangere, Otara and Pukekohe. Dannemora/East Tamaki does not currently offer pickup and drop-off. Transport details can vary by centre, so parents should ask the team about availability, timing and any current fee when booking a visit.

## Does Little Climbers provide meals?

Meals and snacks vary by centre and enrolled hours. Mangere provides morning tea and afternoon tea. Dannemora provides freshly cooked vegetarian meals according to enrolment hours. Otara and Pukekohe also provide food, but the exact meal schedule should be confirmed with the centre before enrolment.

## Does Little Climbers provide nappies?

No. Parents need to provide nappies if their child requires them.

## Does Little Climbers support allergies?

Yes. Allergy support was confirmed in the client discovery call. Parents should discuss allergy details directly with the centre before enrolment so the team can understand the child's needs.

## Does Little Climbers offer 20 Hours ECE?

Yes. Little Climbers offers 20 Hours ECE. In New Zealand, eligible children aged 3, 4 or 5 can receive up to 20 hours of early childhood education funding each week, with daily limits and enrolment requirements. Parents should ask the centre how 20 Hours ECE can apply to their child's enrolment pattern.

## Can Little Climbers help with WINZ childcare subsidy?

Yes. Little Climbers can support parents with the provider section of WINZ childcare subsidy paperwork. Eligibility and subsidy amount depend on Work and Income rules, family income, hours of care and other factors.

## What is FamilyBoost?

FamilyBoost is an Inland Revenue childcare payment that helps eligible caregivers with early childhood education costs. Families claim it through IRD, and eligibility depends on household income, childcare costs and the children cared for. Little Climbers can provide childcare information or invoices/statements where needed, but parents should check IRD's current FamilyBoost rules.

## What age can my child start at Little Climbers?

Age availability depends on the centre. Some Little Climbers centres care for babies from around 3 months, while Mangere focuses on children from about 2 years old and Pukekohe should not be promoted for babies until licensing is confirmed. Parents should choose the centre first, then confirm the age group available there.

## Is daycare good for social development?

Daycare can support social development by helping children build friendships, communicate with other children and adults, settle into routines, and learn how to manage emotions in a group environment. Little Climbers focuses on social skills, communication, self-help skills and confidence, not only early academics.

## What should I do if my child cries at drop-off?

A calm, predictable goodbye usually works better than leaving while your child is distracted. Spend a little time helping your child settle, say goodbye clearly, explain that you are going to work or leaving, and reassure them you will come back. Little Climbers also encourages transition visits to help children become familiar with teachers, routines and the centre environment.

## How long does it take for a child to settle into daycare?

Every child is different. Some children settle quickly, while others need longer and may take weeks or even months. Signs of settling include looking forward to seeing friends or teachers, talking about the centre at home, and sharing home stories with teachers.

## What should parents look for during a childcare centre tour?

Parents should look at the safety of the environment, teacher interactions, routines, resources, the menu, communication systems, curriculum, transition support and how the team responds to their child's age and needs. For babies, parents should also ask about ratios, settling and routines.

## What questions should I ask a childcare centre?

Useful questions include:

- What age groups do you care for?
- What are your hours?
- How do you help children settle?
- What meals are provided?
- Do you offer pickup and drop-off?
- How do you communicate with parents?
- How do you prepare children for school?
- Do you offer 20 Hours ECE?
- Can you help with WINZ subsidy paperwork?
- How do you manage allergies?

## How does Little Climbers prepare children for school?

Little Climbers' transition-to-school approach focuses on communication, social skills, self-help skills, routines, independence, emotional expression and looking after belongings. The goal is to help children feel confident and ready for primary school, not to force early academics before a child is ready.

## Is Little Climbers Montessori or Reggio Emilia?

No. Little Climbers was described in the client meeting as play-based rather than Montessori or Reggio Emilia. The final public wording around Te Whariki/New Zealand ECE curriculum alignment should be confirmed before publishing.

## Do all Little Climbers centres have babies, toddlers and preschoolers?

No. Availability varies by centre. Otara and Dannemora were discussed as offering babies, toddlers and preschoolers. Mangere was discussed as caring for children from about 2 years old. Pukekohe was discussed as 2 to 6 years, with baby care in progress but not yet for public promotion at that time.

## Does Little Climbers offer school holiday programmes?

No. Little Climbers is focused on early childhood care and education rather than school holiday programmes.

## Why choose a small childcare centre?

A smaller centre can make it easier for parents to know the teachers, speak with management, and feel part of the centre community. Little Climbers' small-centre approach supports personal relationships, open communication and a homely environment.
