# PRA Developments - Website Page-by-Page Content Action Plan

**Prepared:** 2026-09-10  
**Input used:** PRA marketing context, meeting action plan, and current public website/indexed page review.  
**Primary context:** [.agents/product-marketing.md](.agents/product-marketing.md)  
**Status:** Working execution plan. Final public copy still needs PRA/Paiman approval and fact checks.

## 1. Main Direction

The website should move from generic SEO copy to a buyer-confidence journey. The strongest message from the marketing context is the **PRA Standard**: quality homes, carefully selected locations, and personal support through the buying process.

Across the website, replace broad claims such as "dream-makers", "best", "biggest", "guaranteed returns", "profits will grow", and generic Christchurch lifestyle copy with specific, verifiable details:

- What PRA builds: new-build homes, townhouses, apartments, and off-plan opportunities.
- Who it helps: first-home buyers and investors first; downsizers secondary.
- Why buyers should trust PRA: family-owned, quality-focused, project-specific specialists, selected locations, clear brochures, show-home access, and aftercare.
- What the buyer should do next: view current properties, download a brochure, watch the relevant walkthrough, ask a question, or book a viewing.

## 9. Specific Copy Rules

Replace this style:

- "Best Property Developers in Christchurch"
- "Biggest Property Developers in NZ"
- "Your investment will flourish"
- "Property values are set to soar"
- "Guaranteed durability, quality, safety and excellence"
- "Dream-makers"
- "This proves how great the returns will be"

Use this style instead:

- "PRA Developments creates quality new-build homes in carefully selected Christchurch locations."
- "The PRA Standard brings together home quality, location quality, and customer support."
- "Each development has its own plans, specifications, pricing, and availability."
- "Investors can request current property information, rental appraisal details where available, and a conversation with the sales team."
- "A show-home visit and project brochure help buyers understand finishes, layouts, and next steps."
